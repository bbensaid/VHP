# Slide 2 — The thesis (again, simply)

American healthcare is structurally failing. Reform must happen across **six pillars together**: Policy, Economics, Technology, Clinical, Equity, Operations.

The HTR system gives you:
- A **book** — the framework, evidence, and practitioner guidance
- A **platform** — the interactive tools, data, and live state intelligence

Used together, they let you do the work.

---

# Slide 3 — Why one without the other doesn't work

**Book without platform:**
- You learn the framework, but cannot test it on real data
- You cannot run risk-stratification scenarios, simulate APMs, score your organization
- You lose currency — health policy changes monthly

**Platform without book:**
- You use the tools without understanding why they're structured the way they are
- You miss the dependency relationships between pillars
- You misread Vermont as "a small state with a big appetite" rather than as the structurally complete case

> Together, they form a complete loop.
