# HTR Chapter 11 Supplement: AHS Restructuring — Research Lab Tool Callouts

## For insertion into Chapter 11 at the indicated section locations

**Editorial note:** Chapter 11 is the most operationally specific chapter in the book — it translates the six-pillar framework directly into an AHS organizational design and implementation roadmap. Two Research Lab tools are directly relevant and should be referenced with sidebar callouts: the Transformation Scorecard (for tracking six-pillar readiness against AHEAD milestones) and the VBC Readiness Assessment (for the 30-dimension organizational gap analysis that AHS must facilitate with each hospital). Insert callouts at the section locations described below.

---

### At Section 3 (The Redesigned AHS Operating Model) or Section 4 (Six-Pillar Framework Applied to AHS):

> **🎯 HTR Research Lab: Transformation Scorecard**
> `/research-lab/knowledge-workspace?tab=scorecard`
>
> Executive six-pillar dashboard with Vermont AHEAD statutory milestones integrated across Policy, Economics, Technology, Clinical, Equity, and Operations pillars. AHS can use the Transformation Scorecard as the tracking framework for its own organizational readiness — and for monitoring aggregate transformation progress across Vermont's 14-hospital system. Act 68's FY2027 RBP and FY2028 global budget deadlines are pre-loaded as milestone markers. The same tool AHS should recommend to hospital boards navigating their own transformation planning processes.

---

### At Section 4 (Six-Pillar Framework Applied to AHS's Restructuring Mandate), after the domain-level analysis:

> **📊 HTR Research Lab: VBC Transformation Readiness Assessment**
> `/research-lab/knowledge-workspace?tab=readiness`
>
> 30-dimension assessment across 6 domains (Strategy & Leadership, Data & Analytics, Clinical Operations, Financial Readiness, Technology Infrastructure, Health Equity) producing a readiness score and prioritized gap analysis. AHS should facilitate this assessment with each of Vermont's 14 hospitals as part of the transformation planning sequence — the gap analysis directly informs which RHRC technical assistance interventions each hospital requires and which RHT Program investment categories should be prioritized for that hospital. Vermont presets: AHEAD Entry (FY2027), Vermont CAH Early Transformation, Advanced System Global Budget Ready.

---

### At Section 5 (AHS Restructuring Implementation Timeline) or Section 6 (Framework for the 2028 Strategic Plan):

> **📖 HTR Research Lab: Evidence Library**
> `/research-lab/knowledge-workspace?tab=evidence`
>
> Search 25 landmark CEA/CUA studies and 20 CMMI innovation model summaries with lesson-learned analysis. AHS's Statewide Strategic Plan requires an evidence base for every proposed intervention category — the Evidence Library provides the foundational research on care management programs, primary care transformation models, and population health interventions that Vermont's 14-hospital system will be implementing. Particularly relevant for the AHS analytical capacity that Oliver Wyman identified as a critical organizational gap.

---

### At the Vermont as National Template section (Chapter 11 conclusion):

> **🏆 HTR Research Lab: Innovation Leaderboard**
> `/research-lab/knowledge-workspace?tab=leaderboard`
>
> Rank all 50 states on a composite health transformation index. Vermont's mandatory all-payer model gives it one of the highest Policy pillar scores nationally. AHS can use the Innovation Leaderboard to benchmark Vermont's transformation progress against peer states — Oregon's reference-based pricing experience, Maryland's global budget model, and Minnesota's multi-payer initiative — and identify what Vermont's national template contribution looks like in quantitative terms as AHEAD data matures.
