### Callout: Hospital Financial Stress Test
> **🏥 HTR Research Lab: Hospital Financial Stress Test**

> Model hospital financial position under concurrent stressors: Act 68 RBP compression, H.R. 1 Medicaid cuts, volume decline, and labor cost escalation. Vermont presets: NVRH (Northeastern VT Regional, CAH), Gifford Medical Center (CAH), Central Vermont Medical Center (Rural PPS), Act 68 RBP Scenario (FY2027), and H.R. 1 Medicaid Cliff (Post-2030). Benchmarks against CAH, Rural PPS, Urban Community, and Urban Tertiary peer groups.

### Callout: HTA Studio

>

### Callout: CEA Calculator

>

### Callout: Actuarial Lab
