## 1. Overview

HTR Advisory is the expert consulting practice that sits above the self-serve platform. While the Subscriber and Professional tiers provide access to tools, data, and AI analysis, the Advisory tier delivers hands-on engagements with HTR analysts who specialize in healthcare transformation across the five analytical pillars.

Advisory clients receive:

- Dedicated analyst engagement (scoped, time-bound)
- Access to all platform features including priority AI (Claude Sonnet 4.6)
- Implementation toolkits and templates tailored to their use case
- Scheduled office hours with pillar-specific experts
- Custom research and analysis not available through the self-serve platform

Advisory engagements complement the platform — advisors use the same Research Lab tools, State Dashboard, and AI Analyst that clients use, but apply deeper domain expertise and custom analysis on top.

## 2. Service Lines

### Policy Strategy

Assists health systems, payers, and state agencies navigating regulatory change and payment reform policy.

**Typical engagements:**

- 1115 waiver design and submission support
- Medicaid managed care contract strategy
- Federal legislative tracking and impact analysis (custom briefings)
- State SPA (State Plan Amendment) strategy

**Primary pillar:** Policy
**Typical duration:** 4–12 weeks
**Client types:** State Medicaid agencies, hospital systems, payer government affairs teams

---

### Value-Based Care Transformation

Guides organizations through APM design, VBC contract negotiation, and care delivery restructuring.

**Typical engagements:**

- APM readiness assessment
- MSSP/ACO REACH strategy and track selection
- Shared savings model design (using APM Design Lab outputs)
- Provider-payer VBC contract review and negotiation support
- Risk stratification implementation planning

**Primary pillar:** Economics
**Typical duration:** 8–24 weeks
**Client types:** Health systems, physician groups, ACOs, regional payers

---

### Health Technology Assessment

Evaluates digital health solutions, AI tools, and clinical technologies for clinical effectiveness, cost-effectiveness, and implementation fit.

**Typical engagements:**

- Vendor evaluation framework design
- Budget impact model construction (using HTA Studio)
- CEA/CUA analysis for new clinical technologies
- AI governance framework for health system AI adoption
- Digital health implementation roadmap

**Primary pillar:** Technology
**Typical duration:** 4–8 weeks
**Client types:** Health system CIOs, pharmacy benefit managers, health tech investors

---

### Clinical Quality & Safety

Supports quality improvement programs, hospital accreditation readiness, and clinical performance optimization.

**Typical engagements:**

- HEDIS performance improvement planning
- CMS Star Rating optimization roadmap
- MIPS scoring strategy for physician practices
- Hospital quality and safety program design
- Joint Commission readiness assessment

**Primary pillar:** Clinical
**Typical duration:** 6–16 weeks
**Client types:** Hospital quality departments, medical groups, health plans

---

### Health Equity Strategy

Designs and implements programs to reduce disparities, address SDOH, and embed equity into clinical and operational processes.

**Typical engagements:**

- Health equity baseline assessment (using Health Equity Studio)
- SDOH screening program design
- Equity-weighted population health program prioritization
- Community health worker program design
- Equity reporting framework (NCQA HES, CMS Health Equity Index)

**Primary pillar:** Equity
**Typical duration:** 8–16 weeks
**Client types:** Health systems, community health centers, Medicaid managed care plans, public health agencies

## 3. Client Segmentation

### Tier A — Health Systems & IDNs

Large integrated delivery networks (300+ beds, or multi-hospital system).

**Typical needs:** VBC transformation, quality program optimization, technology assessment, equity strategy
**Engagement size:** $50k–$250k
**Access level:** Advisory tier

Regional and national health plans, Medicaid managed care organizations, Medicare Advantage plans.

**Typical needs:** APM design, quality improvement, equity programs, provider contracting strategy
**Engagement size:** $30k–$150k
**Access level:** Advisory tier

State Medicaid agencies, Departments of Health, SIM grant offices.

**Typical needs:** Waiver strategy, global budget design, equity policy, AHEAD model implementation
**Engagement size:** $25k–$200k (often grant-funded)
**Access level:** Advisory tier

Multi-specialty groups, primary care networks, ACOs under MSSP or ACO REACH.

**Typical needs:** Risk contract readiness, MIPS optimization, care management design
**Engagement size:** $10k–$50k
**Access level:** Professional or Advisory tier

---

### Tier E — Health Tech & Investors

Digital health companies, private equity, venture capital evaluating healthcare investments.

**Typical needs:** HTA and market analysis, regulatory strategy, clinical evidence review
**Engagement size:** $15k–$75k
**Access level:** Professional or Advisory tier

## 4. Five-Pillar Methodology

Every HTR Advisory engagement is structured around one or more of the five analytical pillars. The pillar framework ensures that:

1. **Analyses are comprehensive** — each pillar provides a distinct lens (policy, economics, technology, clinical, equity)
2. **Cross-pillar dependencies are surfaced** — a VBC transformation engagement touches Economics (payment model), Clinical (care delivery), and Policy (regulatory environment)
3. **Deliverables are standardized** — each pillar has established frameworks, tools, and benchmarks

### Pillar Interaction Map

```text
                    ┌──────────────────┐
                    │     POLICY       │
                    │ Regulatory env.  │
                    │ Payment reform   │
                    └────────┬─────────┘
                             │
          ┌──────────────────┼──────────────────┐
          │                  │                  │
  ┌───────▼──────┐   ┌───────▼──────┐   ┌───────▼──────┐
  │  ECONOMICS   │   │  TECHNOLOGY  │   │   CLINICAL   │
  │ Cost & value │   │ Digital infra│   │ Care delivery│
  │ APMs & VBC   │   │ AI & FHIR    │   │ Quality      │
  └───────┬──────┘   └──────────────┘   └───────┬──────┘
          │                                      │
          └──────────────────┬───────────────────┘
                             │
                    ┌────────▼─────────┐
                    │     EQUITY       │
                    │ SDOH & disparities│
                    │ Access & community│
                    └──────────────────┘
```

Equity sits as a cross-cutting lens — it informs every other pillar rather than existing in isolation.

### Deliverable Standards by Pillar

| Pillar | Standard Deliverables |
| --- | --- |
| **Policy** | Policy landscape brief, regulatory risk matrix, waiver strategy memo |
| **Economics** | Financial model (Excel/Google Sheets), APM design specification, ROI analysis |
| **Technology** | Vendor evaluation scorecard, HTA report, implementation roadmap |
| **Clinical** | Quality gap analysis, HEDIS/Stars improvement plan, workflow recommendations |
| **Equity** | Disparity baseline report, SDOH program design, equity action plan |

## 5. Engagement Model

### Phase 1 — Scoping (Week 1–2)

- Intake form review and discovery call
- Define engagement scope, deliverables, and timeline
- Confirm data access requirements
- Sign engagement letter / SOW

### Phase 2 — Assessment (Weeks 2–4)

- Review client data, contracts, and program materials
- Run relevant Research Lab analyses (HTR tools used directly in client work)
- Stakeholder interviews (if included in scope)
- Identify key gaps, risks, and opportunities

### Phase 3 — Analysis & Recommendations (Weeks 4–8)

- Deep-dive analysis using HTR's proprietary frameworks
- Draft deliverables for client review
- Revise based on feedback (one revision round included)
- Present findings to client leadership

### Phase 4 — Implementation Support (Weeks 8–12+, optional)

- Ongoing advisory support during implementation
- Monthly check-ins and progress review
- Adaptation of recommendations as conditions change
- Final engagement close-out and lessons learned

### What Is Included in Every Engagement

- Access to the full HTR platform (all subscriber features + Research Lab)
- Priority AI Analyst (Claude Sonnet 4.6)
- Dedicated analyst point of contact
- All deliverables in agreed formats
- Office hours availability during the engagement period

## 6. Advisory Hub (Platform)

The `/advisory-hub` page is the platform-side gateway for Advisory and Professional subscribers.

### Features

- **Service Line Browser** — Browse services organized by pillar with descriptions and expected outcomes
- **Intake Form** — Submit an engagement request (name, organization, pillar focus, timeline, goals)
- **Implementation Toolkits** — Download templates, frameworks, and checklists by service line
- **Office Hours Scheduler** — Book a 30-minute slot with a pillar-specific HTR analyst
- **Resource Library** — Advisory-tier-only research briefs and white papers

### Access Gating

The `/advisory-hub` route requires `subscriber` role or above in `middleware.ts`. Toolkit downloads and office hours scheduling require `professional` or `advisory` role (enforced server-side in the relevant API routes).

### Intake Form Data

Submitted intake forms are stored in the `advisory_intakes` table (migration `004_advisory.sql`) and trigger an email notification to the advisory team via Loops.so.

1. Create the toolkit document (Word, Excel, or PDF)
2. In Sanity Studio → Advisory → Toolkits → Add
3. Upload the file, assign a pillar, write a description
4. Publish — it appears in the Advisory Hub toolkit section automatically

## 9. Office Hours

Office Hours are 30-minute scheduled sessions with HTR pillar experts.

### Scheduling

Available to Professional and Advisory subscribers via the Advisory Hub. The scheduler is an embedded Calendly or Cal.com widget (configured in `frontend/app/advisory-hub/page.tsx`).

Each pillar has a dedicated expert calendar:

| Pillar | Expert Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Policy | Medicaid, Medicare, state legislation, waiver strategy |
| Economics | VBC, APMs, shared savings, health economics |
| Technology | Digital health, FHIR, AI governance, EHR strategy |
| Clinical | Quality measures, hospital operations, care management |
| Equity | SDOH, disparity analysis, community health |

### Session Preparation

Advisory team members prepare for office hours by:

1. Reviewing the subscriber's profile (role, organization, pillar interests from onboarding)
2. Checking their recent AI Analyst questions (if they consent to sharing)
3. Pulling relevant state or organization data from the State Dashboard and Research Lab

## 10. Intake & CRM

### Intake Process

1. Subscriber submits intake form at `/advisory-hub`
2. Form data stored in `advisory_intakes` table
3. Loops.so sends notification email to advisory@healthtransformationreview.com
4. Advisory team responds within 2 business days with a discovery call invitation

### CRM Integration

Intake records are managed in the `advisory_intakes` Supabase table. For external CRM sync, an admin can export the table as CSV from the admin dashboard.

Fields captured:

```text
- name
- email
- organization
- organization_type (health system | payer | state agency | physician group | other)
- pillar_focus (multi-select)
- timeline (immediate | 1-3 months | 3-6 months | exploring)
- goals (free text, max 1000 chars)
- budget_range (optional)
- created_at
```

### Confidentiality

All engagement materials, client data, and deliverables are treated as confidential. Advisory subscribers are covered by the platform Terms of Service confidentiality provisions. Custom NDAs are available for engagements involving sensitive state or organizational data.
