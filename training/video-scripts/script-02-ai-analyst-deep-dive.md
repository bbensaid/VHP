# Video Script: AI Analyst Deep Dive (4–5 minutes)
**Health Transformation Review (HTR)**
*For use with Loom, Synthesia, HeyGen, or Camtasia*

---

## Production Notes
- **Duration:** 4–5 minutes
- **Format:** Screen recording with voiceover
- **Audience:** All subscribers
- **Tone:** Practical, demo-focused — show real queries with real results
- **Screen:** Start at `/chat` (full-page AI view)

---

## SCRIPT

---

**[OPENING — 0:00–0:25]**
*(Screen: `/chat` page loaded)*

**NARRATOR:**
The AI Analyst is the most powerful feature on HTR. It's not a chatbot that searches Google — it's a research assistant trained on HTR's full content library: every policy analysis, every state dataset, every research lab methodology.

In this video, I'll show you how to get the most out of it with real examples.

---

**[ACCESSING THE AI — 0:25–0:55]**
*(Screen: Show the right sidebar on a regular platform page, then switch to `/chat`)*

**NARRATOR:**
You can access the AI Analyst in two ways.

The sidebar — always available on the right side of most pages. Great for a quick question while you're reading an analysis.

Or the full-page chat at `/chat` — this is what I recommend for serious research sessions. More space, better focus.

*(Screen: Role selection — show the Welcome page briefly)*

When you set your role at the Welcome page, the AI opens with a personalized greeting and starter questions for your profession. I'm going to work from the executive role today.

---

**[DEMO 1: RESEARCH QUESTION — 0:55–2:00]**
*(Screen: Type in the chat)*

**NARRATOR:**
Let me ask a real question an executive might bring.

*(Type: "What are the financial risks of transitioning to a global hospital budget, and how has Vermont's experience with AHEAD compared to Maryland's all-payer model?")*

*(Pause — let the response generate)*

Notice a few things. The AI is drawing specifically from HTR's Vermont coverage, the AHEAD Model page, and its economics content — not pulling generic results.

*(Highlight key parts of the response)*

It gives a structured answer: context, comparison, key risks. I can ask it to go deeper on any point.

*(Type: "Go deeper on the risk corridors in the AHEAD Model")*

This follow-up conversation is where the real research value comes in. You're not submitting a new query each time — you're building on a thread.

---

**[DEMO 2: NAVIGATION ASSISTANCE — 2:00–2:40]**
*(Screen: Ask a navigation question)*

**NARRATOR:**
Here's something most users don't realize — the AI can navigate you to specific tools.

*(Type: "What tool on HTR would help me model the financial impact of a Medicaid rate cut on my hospital?")*

*(Response appears — it directs to the Hospital Financial Stress Test)*

It tells me exactly where to go. I can click the link it provides and go directly there without searching.

---

**[DEMO 3: DRAFTING SUPPORT — 2:40–3:30]**
*(Screen: Ask a drafting question)*

**NARRATOR:**
The AI Analyst is also excellent for drafting.

*(Type: "Draft a one-paragraph board-level summary of the H.R. 1 Medicaid cliff risk for a Vermont hospital CFO")*

*(Response appears)*

This is ready to paste into a board presentation with minor edits. The AI knows the Vermont context, knows what a CFO cares about, and writes at the right level.

You can iterate: *"Make it shorter"*, *"Add a specific dollar figure"*, *"Make it more urgent in tone"* — just keep the conversation going.

---

**[TIPS FOR BETTER RESULTS — 3:30–4:15]**
*(Screen: Show the chat interface while narrating)*

**NARRATOR:**
A few tips to get better answers.

**First: add context.** Instead of "What is AHEAD?" try "I'm a CFO at a 200-bed Vermont hospital — what does AHEAD mean for our reimbursement?"

**Second: ask for a specific format.** "Give me this as a bulleted list for a slide." "Explain this to a non-economist."

**Third: use it as a guide.** Ask "Where on HTR can I find more detail on this?" and it will point you to the right page or tool.

**Fourth: don't start over.** Keep your research conversation going. The AI remembers what you've discussed in the session — build on it.

---

**[VOICE MODE — 4:15–4:40]**
*(Screen: Show the microphone button)*

**NARRATOR:**
One more feature: voice mode. Press Command-Shift-V on a Mac to switch to voice input. Speak your question. The AI responds in audio.

This is great for hands-free research — when you're reviewing documents, or when typing is inconvenient.

---

**[CLOSING — 4:40–5:00]**
*(Screen: Chat interface)*

**NARRATOR:**
The AI Analyst gets more useful the more specific you are with it. Start with your actual question, give it your context, and follow up until you have what you need.

The next video covers the Research Lab — the quantitative modeling side of HTR.

---

## Post-Production Notes
- Use real screen recordings of actual queries where possible — live responses are more credible than scripted text
- Zoom in on the chat text so it's readable at 1080p
- Pause on responses long enough for viewers to read the first 2–3 lines before the narrator continues
