# Video Script: Platform Overview (3–4 minutes)
**Health Transformation Review (HTR)**
*For use with Loom, Synthesia, HeyGen, or Camtasia*

---

## Production Notes
- **Duration:** 3–4 minutes
- **Format:** Screen recording with voiceover OR avatar + slides
- **Audience:** All new users
- **Tone:** Confident, clear, not overly formal
- **Screen:** Start on the homepage (`/`), then navigate as scripted

---

## SCRIPT

---

**[OPENING — 0:00–0:20]**
*(Screen: HTR homepage)*

**NARRATOR:**
Welcome to Health Transformation Review — the intelligence platform for healthcare leaders, policy analysts, clinicians, economists, and researchers working at the intersection of policy, finance, technology, and clinical care.

In the next few minutes, I'll show you what's here, how it's organized, and how to get to what matters most to you.

---

**[THE SIX PILLARS — 0:20–1:00]**
*(Screen: Hover over the navigation, show the six pillars)*

**NARRATOR:**
The entire platform is built around six pillars — Policy, Economics, Technology, Clinical, Equity, and Operations. These aren't just categories. They're an integrated framework that reflects how healthcare actually works: policy shapes economics, technology enables clinical care, and equity cuts across all of it.

*(Screen: Click to `/about/framework`)*

Every piece of content, every tool, and every analysis on HTR maps to one or more of these pillars. This framework is your navigation map.

---

**[THE AI ANALYST — 1:00–1:40]**
*(Screen: Expand the AI Analyst sidebar)*

**NARRATOR:**
The fastest way to use HTR isn't to browse — it's to ask.

The AI Analyst, here on the right side of the screen, knows the entire platform. You can ask it research questions, ask it where to find a specific tool, or ask it to explain a policy in plain language.

*(Screen: Type a question — e.g., "What are the key risks of a global budget transition for a rural hospital?")*

It draws from HTR's content library to give you answers grounded in real analysis — not generic internet results.

You can also use it full-screen at `/chat`.

---

**[THE WELCOME PAGE & PERSONALIZATION — 1:40–2:10]**
*(Screen: Navigate to `/welcome`)*

**NARRATOR:**
When you first arrive — or any time you want to reset — come to the Welcome page.

Select your role: executive, clinician, policy analyst, economist, tech professional, compliance officer, researcher, or investor.

*(Screen: Click "Health System Executive")*

Your role personalizes the AI Analyst's starter questions and surfaces the content most relevant to your work. It takes five seconds and you can change it any time.

---

**[RESEARCH LAB — 2:10–2:45]**
*(Screen: Navigate to `/research-lab`)*

**NARRATOR:**
For quantitative work, the Research Lab has 21 interactive modeling tools.

Need to design a payment model? The APM Design Lab. Need to model Medicaid enrollment under work requirements? The Work Requirements Calculator. Need to stress-test a hospital's financials? The Hospital Financial Stress Test.

*(Screen: Click into one tab, show the interface briefly)*

Each tool lets you input your own parameters and get actionable outputs. The AI Analyst in the sidebar can help interpret your results.

---

**[ACADEMY & COMMUNITY — 2:45–3:15]**
*(Screen: Navigate to `/academy`, then briefly to `/connect`)*

**NARRATOR:**
HTR Academy offers courses, webinars, case studies, and a 500-term glossary — all aligned to the six pillars. Start with Personalized Learning and let the AI build a curriculum based on your role and goals.

*(Screen: Navigate to `/connect`)*

And HTR Connect is the community hub — cohorts, office hours with domain experts, pillar-specific forums, and a direct line to the HTR advisory team.

---

**[CLOSING — 3:15–3:40]**
*(Screen: Back to homepage or Welcome page)*

**NARRATOR:**
That's the overview. The best way to start is to go to the Welcome page, set your role, and let the AI Analyst guide you in.

In the next videos, we'll go deep on specific features: the Research Lab tools, the AI Analyst, voice navigation, and the Academy.

Welcome to HTR.
