# Video Script: Getting Started — Your First 5 Minutes (2 minutes)
**Health Transformation Review (HTR)**
*For use with Loom, Synthesia, HeyGen, or Camtasia*

---

## Production Notes
- **Duration:** 2 minutes (tight — this is the onboarding video)
- **Format:** Screen recording OR avatar on slides — keep energy up
- **Audience:** Brand new users, first login
- **Tone:** Warm, fast-paced, reassuring
- **Placement:** Embed in welcome email, onboarding flow, and Academy Getting Started page
- **Screen:** Start at `/welcome`

---

## SCRIPT

---

**[0:00–0:15]**
*(Screen: `/welcome` page)*

**NARRATOR:**
You're on HTR. This is the Welcome page. It's the best place to start — and it takes ten seconds.

Tell us who you are. Click your role.

*(Screen: Click "Hospital / Health System Executive")*

---

**[0:15–0:35]**
*(Screen: `/chat` opens with personalized greeting)*

**NARRATOR:**
You're now in the AI Analyst — personalized for your role. This is your guide.

Ask it anything. Where to find a tool. What a regulation means. How to use a feature.

*(Screen: Type "What should I look at first on HTR as a hospital executive?")*

It knows the whole platform.

---

**[0:35–1:05]**
*(Screen: Navigate to sidebar on a content page)*

**NARRATOR:**
While the AI is your guide, here's the lay of the land.

Six pillars in the top navigation: Policy, Economics, Technology, Clinical, Equity, Operations. Each one has in-depth analysis and connects to the Research Lab tools.

*(Screen: Flash to `/research-lab` briefly)*

The Research Lab — 21 modeling tools for everything from APM design to Medicaid eligibility.

*(Screen: Flash to `/academy` briefly)*

The Academy — courses, webinars, case studies, and a 500-term glossary.

*(Screen: Flash to `/dashboard` briefly)*

And the 50-State Dashboard — hospital and health system data for every state.

---

**[1:05–1:35]**
*(Screen: Return to AI chat)*

**NARRATOR:**
You don't need to explore all of this to get value on day one. The AI Analyst will take you exactly where you need to go.

Try asking it your first real question — whatever brought you to HTR.

*(Type: "I'm trying to understand what Vermont's global budget model means for rural hospitals")*

*(Let response generate briefly)*

---

**[1:35–2:00]**
*(Screen: Bookmark icon visible on a page)*

**NARRATOR:**
One last thing — bookmark the pages and tools you return to. Click the bookmark icon on any page and it saves to My Library.

That's it. You're ready.

The full guide for your role is in the Academy's Getting Started section — but the best way to learn HTR is to use it.

Welcome.

---

## Post-Production Notes
- This is the highest-priority video — used in welcome emails and in-app onboarding
- Keep pacing fast — cut dead air ruthlessly
- Subtitles required — many users will watch without audio
- Mobile-friendly export: ensure text is readable at small sizes
