# Feature Guide: Voice Interface
**Health Transformation Review (HTR) Platform**

---

## What Is the Voice Interface?

HTR's Voice Interface lets you navigate the platform, search for content, and interact with the AI Analyst entirely hands-free. It combines speech-to-text input with text-to-speech AI responses.

**Activation:** Press **⌘⇧V** (Mac) or the microphone button in the UI

---

## Browser Support

| Browser | Support |
|---------|---------|
| Chrome / Chromium | Full support (recommended) |
| Edge | Full support |
| Safari | Partial (Web Speech API limitations) |
| Firefox | Limited |

Voice features require microphone permission. When prompted, click **Allow**.

---

## Activating Voice

### Keyboard Shortcut
Press **⌘⇧V** (Command + Shift + V on Mac) from anywhere on the platform.

### UI Button
Look for the microphone icon in:
- The AI Analyst chat input area
- The top navigation bar (on supported pages)

### From the Chat Page
Navigate to `/chat` — the voice toggle is prominently displayed in the chat interface.

---

## What You Can Do With Voice

### 1. Ask the AI Analyst
Speak your question naturally. The AI responds in audio (text-to-speech):
- *"What are the key requirements of Vermont Act 167?"*
- *"Summarize the global budget transition modeler results"*
- *"What tools does HTR have for Medicaid eligibility analysis?"*

### 2. Navigate the Platform
Speak navigation commands:
- *"Go to the Research Lab"*
- *"Open the 50-State Dashboard"*
- *"Take me to the Vermont Medicaid page"*
- *"Open the FHIR lab"*

### 3. Search
- *"Search for hospital-at-home"*
- *"Find content about Vermont Act 68"*
- *"Search for APM design tools"*

### 4. Control the Interface
- *"Scroll down"*
- *"Open the AI chat"*
- *"Go back"*
- *"Bookmark this page"*

---

## Voice Interaction Tips

| Tip | Details |
|-----|---------|
| Speak clearly | The platform uses your browser's speech recognition engine |
| Pause before speaking | Wait for the microphone indicator to show it's listening |
| Use natural language | You don't need special command syntax |
| Quiet environment | Background noise reduces accuracy |
| Follow-up questions | The AI Analyst maintains context between voice turns |

---

## Text-to-Speech (AI Audio Responses)

When voice mode is active, AI Analyst responses are read aloud. You can:
- Interrupt the AI at any time by speaking
- Press **⌘⇧V** to toggle voice off and return to text-only mode
- Adjust your system volume to control audio output

Voice quality and speed depend on your browser and operating system's TTS engine.

---

## Use Cases for Voice

**Hands-free research** — Review platform content while taking notes by hand or in another application

**Commute / travel** — Listen to AI analysis while driving or on the go (use responsibly)

**Accessibility** — Full platform functionality for users who prefer or require voice interaction

**Multi-tasking** — Query the AI Analyst while working in another tool without switching windows

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|---------|
| Microphone not working | Check browser permissions (Site Settings > Microphone) |
| AI not responding to voice | Ensure you see the "listening" indicator before speaking |
| No audio from AI | Check system volume; try toggling voice off and back on |
| Voice not activating with ⌘⇧V | Ensure the HTR browser tab is focused |
| Recognition errors | Speak more slowly; use Chrome for best accuracy |

---

## Deactivating Voice

- Press **⌘⇧V** again to toggle off
- Click the microphone button to turn it off
- Navigate away from the page (voice resets per page)
