# Feature Guide: HTR Academy
**Health Transformation Review (HTR) Platform**

---

## What Is HTR Academy?

HTR Academy is the platform's education center. It provides structured learning — courses, webinars, case studies, learning tracks, and a glossary — built on the same Six-Pillar Framework that organizes the rest of the platform.

**Entry point:** `/academy`

---

## Academy Sections

### 1. Personalized Learning — `/academy/personalized-learning`
An AI-driven engine that builds a custom learning path based on:
- Your selected role (executive, clinician, policy analyst, etc.)
- Your stated knowledge gaps and goals
- Your progress through existing content

**How to use:**
1. Navigate to `/academy/personalized-learning`
2. Answer the short intake questions about your role and goals
3. Receive a customized curriculum with prioritized content
4. Your path updates as you complete content and as new material is published

---

### 2. Learning Tracks — `/academy/tracks`
Structured multi-course sequences for specific professional roles. Each track culminates in a verifiable credential.

**Current tracks include:**
- Health Economist Track
- Compliance Officer Track
- CMO / Clinical Leader Track
- Policy Analyst Track
- Health Tech Leader Track

**How to use:**
1. Browse tracks at `/academy/tracks`
2. Click a track to see the full curriculum and estimated time commitment
3. Enroll and complete modules in sequence
4. Access your credential upon completion from `/account/courses`

---

### 3. Courses — `/academy/courses`
Individual courses on specific topics. Each course contains multiple modules with content, exercises, and assessments.

**How to use:**
1. Browse at `/academy/courses` — filter by pillar, difficulty, or topic
2. Click a course to see the syllabus, faculty, and estimated time
3. Enroll — your enrollment is tracked at `/account/courses`
4. Access individual modules at `/academy/modules/[slug]`
5. Download certificates upon completion

---

### 4. Webinars — `/academy/webinars`
Live and recorded expert-led sessions. Webinars bring in domain practitioners for structured presentations and live Q&A.

**How to use:**
1. Browse upcoming and past webinars at `/academy/webinars`
2. Register for upcoming webinars (subscribers only)
3. Access recordings immediately after the live session
4. Individual webinar pages include slides, transcripts, and AI-generated summaries

---

### 5. Case Studies — `/academy/case-studies`
Real-world analyses of healthcare transformation initiatives. Case studies cover:
- APM implementations and their financial outcomes
- State Medicaid waiver programs (what worked, what didn't)
- AI and digital health deployments in health systems
- Equity initiative design and outcomes
- Vermont-specific program analyses

**How to use:**
1. Browse at `/academy/case-studies`
2. Filter by pillar, geography, or topic
3. Each case study includes background, key decisions, outcomes, and lessons learned
4. The AI Analyst can reference case studies when answering your questions

---

### 6. Glossary — `/academy/glossary`
Searchable definitions for 500+ healthcare policy, economics, clinical, and technology terms. Authoritative definitions maintained by HTR's research team.

**How to use:**
- Search by term using the search bar
- Browse alphabetically or by pillar
- Click any term for the full definition with contextual examples
- Use it alongside AI Analyst sessions when unfamiliar terms appear

---

### 7. Faculty Directory — `/academy/faculty`
Profiles of the domain experts who produce HTR Academy content — their credentials, areas of expertise, and associated courses and webinars.

---

### 8. Medicaid Learning Center — `/academy/medicaid`
A dedicated sub-section for Medicaid-specific education:
- **Medicaid Learning Center** (`/academy/medicaid`) — overview, modules, and resources
- **Medicaid Glossary** (`/academy/medicaid/glossary`) — Medicaid-specific terminology

**Best for:** Compliance officers, state Medicaid staff, enrollment navigators, policy analysts

---

## Tracking Your Progress

All Academy activity is tracked in your account:
- **My Courses** (`/account/courses`) — enrolled courses, completion status, certificates
- **My Library** (`/saved`) — bookmarked content including case studies and webinars
- **Personalized Learning** — your AI-generated curriculum updates as you complete content

---

## Academy Tips

| Tip | Details |
|-----|---------|
| Start with Personalized Learning | Get a prioritized curriculum instead of browsing randomly |
| Use the Glossary alongside AI chats | When the AI uses an unfamiliar term, look it up immediately |
| Bookmark webinar recordings | Recordings are available immediately — save them to My Library |
| Combine case studies with tools | After reading a case study, model a similar scenario in the Research Lab |
| Follow a Learning Track for credentials | Tracks give you a credential — individual courses don't |
