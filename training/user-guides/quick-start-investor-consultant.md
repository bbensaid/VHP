# Quick-Start Guide: Investor / Consultant
**Health Transformation Review (HTR) Platform**

---

## Your Role on HTR

As a healthcare investor or consultant, HTR gives you:
- Market intelligence on consolidation, M&A activity, and investment trends
- Financial modeling and benchmarking tools
- State-level hospital and market data
- AI-powered research for due diligence and client work

---

## Step 1: Set Your Role

At **/welcome**, click **"Investor / Consultant"**.

The AI will open ready to help with market trends, M&A activity, financial benchmarks, or any deal or engagement question you bring.

---

## Step 2: Your 5 Most Valuable Destinations

### 1. Economics Hub — `/economics`
Market intelligence layer:
- **Market & Finance** (`/economics/market`) — consolidation, payer-provider dynamics, capital flows
- **Value-Based Care Models** (`/economics/value`) — APM mechanics, risk transfer economics
- **Healthcare Investment Trends** (`/economics/investment`) — venture, private equity, government spending patterns
- **Labor & Workforce** (`/economics/cea`) — labor economics affecting deal assumptions

### 2. Investment Tracker — `/investment-tracker`
Track healthcare investment activity: venture rounds, PE acquisitions, strategic M&A, government grant programs. Filter by sector, geography, and deal type.

### 3. 50-State Dashboard — `/dashboard`
Market mapping tool: hospital landscape, payer mix, Medicaid penetration, and financial health metrics across all states. Essential for market entry analysis and portfolio monitoring.

### 4. HTR Transformation Readiness Simulator — `/htr-simulator`
Run a readiness assessment on a target or client organization. Use it to identify operational and financial vulnerabilities before an engagement or deal.

### 5. Research Lab: Payment Models — `/research-lab/payment-models`
- **APM Design Lab** — understand contract structures in target markets
- **Shared Savings Calculator** — project financial performance of VBC arrangements
- **Global Budget Transition Modeler** — assess state-level budget reform trajectories
- **Hospital Financial Stress Test** (`/research-lab/policy-quality?tab=scorecard`) — model reimbursement risk on specific targets

## Step 3: Key Workflows

### Market Entry Analysis
1. Use the **50-State Dashboard** to assess market structure in target geography
2. Check **Market & Finance** analysis at `/economics/market`
3. Review state program pages (e.g., CalAIM, Oregon CCO, AHEAD Model) for regulatory environment
4. Ask the AI: *"What is the competitive and regulatory landscape for health systems in [state]?"*
5. Check the **Investment Tracker** for recent deal activity in the space

### Target / Client Due Diligence
1. Run the **HTR Transformation Readiness Simulator** for organizational assessment
2. Pull the target's state data from the **50-State Dashboard**
3. Use the **Hospital Financial Stress Test** to model reimbursement risk
4. Ask the AI: *"What are the key financial risks for a rural hospital in Vermont operating under global budget constraints?"*
5. Review **Operations hub** for workforce and revenue cycle benchmarks

### Sector Intelligence for Fund or Client
1. Set policy alerts at `/connect/alerts` for sectors you monitor
2. Check **The Wire** (`/the-wire`) daily for news
3. Ask the AI weekly: *"What are the most significant policy and market developments in digital health / VBC / Medicaid this week?"*
4. Bookmark key analysis pages to **My Library** for client reporting

### VBC Contract Valuation
1. Go to **APM Design Lab** (`/research-lab/payment-models?tab=apm-design`)
2. Model the contract structure and risk corridor
3. Run **Shared Savings Calculator** (`?tab=apm-calc`) for financial projections
4. Model provider financial impact via **Hospital Financial Stress Test**
5. Validate population assumptions with the **Population Health Modeler**
