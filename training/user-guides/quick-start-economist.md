# Quick-Start Guide: Health Economist / Actuary
**Health Transformation Review (HTR) Platform**

## Your Role on HTR

As a health economist or actuary, HTR gives you:
- Quantitative modeling tools for APM design, cost-effectiveness analysis, and actuarial projections
- Deep economics intelligence on value-based care, market dynamics, and investment trends
- AI-powered research assistant for complex financial and policy questions
- State-level financial and utilization data across all 50 states

## Step 1: Set Your Role

At **/welcome**, click **"Health Economist / Actuary"**.

The AI will open ready to assist with cost-effectiveness modeling, APM design, actuarial analysis, or any quantitative healthcare finance question.

## Step 2: Your 5 Most Valuable Destinations

### 1. Research Lab: Payment Models — `/research-lab/payment-models`
The core toolkit for payment model work. Four tools:
- **APM Design Lab** (`?tab=apm-design`) — design alternative payment models: MSSP, bundled payments, direct contracting
- **Shared Savings Calculator** (`?tab=apm-calc`) — project shared savings/losses under different benchmark assumptions
- **CEA Calculator** (`?tab=cea`) — cost-effectiveness analysis with QALY and ICER calculations
- **Global Budget Transition Modeler** (`?tab=gb-transition`) — model a state's transition to a global hospital budget

### 2. Actuarial Lab — `/research-lab/policy-quality?tab=actuarial`
Full actuarial modeling environment: coverage scenarios, cost trend projections, utilization assumptions, premium/capitation rate development. Model the impact of benefit changes, population shifts, or policy interventions.

### 3. Economics Hub — `/economics`
In-depth analysis across:
- **Value-Based Care Models** (`/economics/value`) — financial mechanics, risk types, outcomes data
- **Market & Finance** (`/economics/market`) — M&A, payer consolidation, capital markets
- **Labor & Workforce Strategy** (`/economics/cea`) — labor cost trends, workforce economics
- **Healthcare Investment Trends** (`/economics/investment`) — venture, private equity, government spending

### 4. Hospital Financial Stress Test — `/research-lab/policy-quality?tab=scorecard`
Model how policy or payment system changes hit hospital margins. Adjust reimbursement rates, volume assumptions, and cost structures. Essential for Medicaid rate-setting and APM feasibility analysis.

### 5. 50-State Dashboard — `/dashboard`
Access state-level utilization, spending, and hospital financial data. Use **Compare** (`/dashboard/compare`) for cross-state econometric benchmarking.

## Step 3: Key Workflows

### Designing an APM
1. Go to **APM Design Lab** (`/research-lab/payment-models?tab=apm-design`)
2. Select model type, risk arrangement, quality metric set
3. Run the **Shared Savings Calculator** to project financial impact
4. Ask the AI: *"What historical performance data exists for MSSP ACOs in rural states?"*
5. Validate with the **Hospital Financial Stress Test** from the provider's perspective

### Cost-Effectiveness Analysis
1. Open the **CEA Calculator** (`/research-lab/payment-models?tab=cea`)
2. Enter intervention costs, outcome data, comparator
3. Calculate ICER against standard thresholds ($50K, $100K, $150K per QALY)
4. Ask the AI for supporting literature: *"What are the published ICER benchmarks for hospital-at-home programs?"*

### State Global Budget Modeling
1. Go to **Global Budget Transition Modeler** (`?tab=gb-transition`)
2. Set baseline hospital spending, growth assumptions, and benchmark methodology
3. Model transition timelines and savings projections
4. Cross-reference with Vermont's AHEAD Model data (`/ahead-model`)

### Medicaid Actuarial Analysis
1. Use the **Actuarial Lab** for capitation rate development
2. Check **Vermont Medicaid** (`/vermont-medicaid`) for state-specific program parameters
3. Run the **Medicaid Eligibility Simulator** (`/medicaid-eligibility-simulator`) to model population changes
4. Use the **Work Requirements Calculator** (`/research-lab/policy-quality?tab=medicaid-wr`) to model enrollment impacts
