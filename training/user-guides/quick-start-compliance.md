# Quick-Start Guide: Medicaid / Compliance Officer
**Health Transformation Review (HTR) Platform**

---

## Your Role on HTR

As a Medicaid or compliance officer, HTR gives you:
- Authoritative coverage of Medicaid rules, federal mandates, and state program requirements
- Simulation tools for eligibility, work requirements, and program impact modeling
- Real-time regulatory alerts and rule tracking
- AI-powered research for compliance questions

---

## Step 1: Set Your Role

At **/welcome**, click **"Medicaid / Compliance Officer"**.

The AI will open ready to help with Vermont Medicaid eligibility rules, Act 167 compliance requirements, regulatory updates, or any compliance question you raise.

## Step 2: Your 5 Most Valuable Destinations

### 1. Vermont Medicaid — `/vermont-medicaid`
Complete coverage of Vermont's Medicaid program: eligibility rules, covered populations, benefit structure, waiver programs, and current policy status.

### 2. Medicaid Eligibility Simulator — `/medicaid-eligibility-simulator`
Test eligibility determinations across different income levels, household compositions, and categorical groups. Essential for:
- Staff training on eligibility rules
- Modeling impact of proposed rule changes
- Constituent casework support

### 3. Policy Hub: Regulation — `/policy/regulation`
Track federal regulatory activity: CMS proposed and final rules, FHHS mandates, and congressional action affecting Medicaid and compliance requirements.

### 4. Compliance Hub — `/operations/compliance`
Integrated coverage of quality, compliance, and risk management across healthcare organizations. Includes HIPAA, anti-kickback, Stark Law, and value-based care compliance considerations.

### 5. Vermont Program Pages
- **Vermont Act 167** (`/vermont-act-167`) — 2022 healthcare transformation compliance requirements
- **Vermont Act 68** (`/vermont-act-68`) — 2025 updates and obligations
- **Vermont RHT Program** (`/vermont-rht-program`) — Rural Health Transformation participation requirements
- **CMS AHEAD Model** (`/ahead-model`) — Vermont's participation and obligations

## Step 3: Key Workflows

### Researching an Eligibility Question
1. Start with the **Medicaid Eligibility Simulator** to test the specific scenario
2. Cross-reference with **Vermont Medicaid** for authoritative program rules
3. Ask the AI: *"What are the income eligibility rules for Vermont Medicaid for a family of 3 with mixed immigration status?"*
4. Check `/policy/regulation` for any recent rule changes affecting eligibility

### Monitoring Regulatory Changes
1. Set up **Policy Alerts** at `/connect/alerts` — select Regulation, Medicaid, and Compliance topics
2. Check **The Wire** (`/the-wire`) daily for regulatory news
3. Ask the AI weekly: *"What are the most significant Medicaid regulatory changes in the past 30 days?"*
4. Save key rule pages to **My Library** (`/saved`) for quick reference

### Assessing Vermont Act 167 / Act 68 Compliance
1. Read the **Act 167 overview** at `/vermont-act-167`
2. Use the **Act 167 Simulator** (`/vermont-act-167/simulator`) to model compliance scenarios
3. Repeat for **Act 68** (`/vermont-act-68`) and its simulator
4. Ask the AI: *"What reporting requirements does Act 167 impose on critical access hospitals?"*

### Work Requirements Analysis
1. Go to **Work Requirements Calculator** (`/research-lab/policy-quality?tab=medicaid-wr`)
2. Model enrollment impact under different work requirement structures
3. Analyze disenrollment projections by population segment
4. Cross-reference with **H.R. 1 Cliff Scenario** (`?tab=hr1-cliff`) for federal context

### Staff Training on Eligibility
1. Use the **Medicaid Eligibility Simulator** as a training tool — run scenarios with staff
2. Build case studies around real eligibility questions your team encounters
3. Use the **Medicaid Learning Center** (`/academy/medicaid`) for structured education
4. Access the **Medicaid Glossary** (`/academy/medicaid/glossary`) for terminology training
