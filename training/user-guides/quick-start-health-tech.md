# Quick-Start Guide: Health Tech Professional
**Health Transformation Review (HTR) Platform**

---

## Your Role on HTR

As a health technology professional — working in health IT, digital health product development, AI in healthcare, or FHIR/interoperability — HTR gives you:
- Technical intelligence on AI governance, FHIR standards, digital health ROI, and cybersecurity
- Hands-on research tools for interoperability and AI governance analysis
- API access to HTR's data and AI capabilities
- Developer documentation and integration resources

---

## Step 1: Set Your Role

At **/welcome**, click **"Health Tech Professional"**.

The AI will open ready for AI governance questions, FHIR interoperability issues, digital health ROI analysis, or any technical healthcare topic.

## Step 2: Your 5 Most Valuable Destinations

### 1. Technology Hub — `/technology`
The primary intelligence layer for health tech:
- **AI & Machine Learning** (`/technology/ai`) — AI adoption, governance, CMS AI rules
- **Digital Health & Telemedicine** (`/technology/digital`) — digital health regulation, reimbursement, market
- **Data Security & Governance** (`/technology/security`) — HIPAA, cyber threats, data governance frameworks
- **Tech-Enabled Workflow** (`/technology/workflow`) — EHR optimization, clinical workflow tools

### 2. FHIR Interoperability Lab — `/research-lab/interoperability?tab=fhir`
Test and model FHIR R4 data exchange scenarios. Explore HL7 FHIR resource types, CMS interoperability rule compliance, and API design patterns for healthcare data exchange.

### 3. AI Clinical Governance Lab — `/research-lab/technology-ai?tab=ai`
Explore AI governance frameworks for clinical settings: model validation, bias testing, FDA SaMD requirements, CMS coverage policies. Build governance checklists for AI deployment.

### 4. Digital Health Lab — `/research-lab/technology-ai?tab=digital`
Analyze digital health product ROI, reimbursement pathways (remote patient monitoring, digital therapeutics), and regulatory clearance requirements.

### 5. Developer Hub — `/developers`
API documentation, authentication, and integration guides. Generate and manage API keys at `/account/api-keys`. Access HTR's data endpoints for research and integration.

## Step 3: Key Workflows

### FHIR Implementation Analysis
1. Go to the **FHIR Interoperability Lab** (`/research-lab/interoperability?tab=fhir`)
2. Explore CMS interoperability rule requirements (Patient Access API, Provider Directory API)
3. Ask the AI: *"What are the CMS Information Blocking rule requirements for EHR vendors in 2025?"*
4. Read in-depth coverage at **Technology > Digital Health** (`/technology/digital`)

### AI Governance Framework
1. Open the **AI Clinical Governance Lab** (`/research-lab/technology-ai?tab=ai`)
2. Work through the FDA SaMD classification framework
3. Build a governance checklist for your AI tool
4. Ask the AI: *"What are the key requirements in ONC's HTI-2 rule for AI decision support?"*
5. Read **Technology > AI & Machine Learning** for policy context

### Digital Health Product Positioning
1. Use the **Digital Health Lab** (`/research-lab/technology-ai?tab=digital`)
2. Map reimbursement pathways: CPT codes for RPM, digital therapeutics coverage criteria
3. Model ROI with the financial tools under Research Lab > Payment Models
4. Check **The Wire** for FDA clearance news and CMS coverage decisions

### Risk Stratification Tool Development
1. Go to **Risk Stratification Engine** (`/research-lab/interoperability?tab=risk`)
2. Explore clinical risk scoring models and their data requirements
3. Cross-reference with **Population Health Modeler** (`/research-lab/population-equity?tab=population`)
4. Ask the AI about FHIR data elements needed for your model
