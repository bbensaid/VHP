# Quick-Start Guide: Student / Researcher
**Health Transformation Review (HTR) Platform**

---

## Your Role on HTR

As a student or researcher in health policy, economics, public health, or related fields, HTR gives you:
- Comprehensive, practitioner-level analysis across all six healthcare pillars
- Quantitative research tools for modeling and analysis
- A curated evidence library and glossary
- AI-powered research assistance trained on healthcare intelligence

---

## Step 1: Set Your Role

At **/welcome**, click **"Student / Researcher"**.

The AI will open ready to help you explore health policy, economics, technology, clinical outcomes, equity, or operations — whatever your research focus.

---

## Step 2: Orientation: The Six-Pillar Framework

Everything on HTR is organized around six pillars. Understanding this framework is your foundation.

Visit **/about/framework** for the interactive Six-Pillar Framework Map.

| Pillar | Focus | Hub Route |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Policy | Legislation, regulation, mandates | `/policy` |
| Economics | Value-based care, finance, markets | `/economics` |
| Technology | AI, digital health, FHIR, security | `/technology` |
| Clinical | Care innovation, population health | `/clinical` |
| Equity | SDOH, access, algorithmic bias | `/equity` |
| Operations | Revenue cycle, workforce, compliance | `/operations` |

Each pillar has a hub page + 3–5 deep-dive sub-topic pages + dedicated Research Lab tools.

## Step 3: Your 5 Most Valuable Destinations

### 1. Evidence Library — `/research-lab/knowledge-workspace?tab=evidence`
Curated research literature across all six pillars. Peer-reviewed studies, CMS technical reports, government white papers, and practitioner analyses. Searchable and filterable by topic.

### 2. AI Analyst — `/chat`
Your research accelerator. Ask any healthcare policy question in plain language:
- *"What is the evidence base for hospital-at-home programs?"*
- *"Summarize the academic debate on whether Medicaid expansion improved population health."*
- *"What are the methodological strengths and weaknesses of Health Technology Assessment in the US vs. UK?"*

### 3. HTR Glossary — `/academy/glossary`
Authoritative definitions for 500+ healthcare policy and industry terms. Searchable. Essential for anyone new to health policy, economics, or clinical terminology.

### 4. Research Lab — `/research-lab`
21 interactive modeling tools for hands-on research. For researchers, the most useful:
- **Policy Simulator** — model legislation impact
- **CEA Calculator** — cost-effectiveness methodology
- **Health Equity Studio** — equity gap analysis
- **Population Health Modeler** — disease burden and intervention modeling
- **Actuarial Lab** — coverage and cost projections

### 5. Case Studies — `/academy/case-studies`
Real-world analyses of APM implementations, state waiver programs, AI deployments, and equity initiatives. Excellent for literature review and understanding the practitioner perspective.

## Step 4: Research Workflows

### Literature Review on a Policy Topic
1. Start with the AI Analyst: *"Give me an overview of the research on [topic] and the key debates"*
2. Go to the **Evidence Library** and search your topic
3. Read HTR's in-depth analysis in the relevant pillar hub
4. Check **Case Studies** for real-world examples
5. Ask the AI for citations and further reading

### Quantitative Policy Analysis
1. Use the **Policy Simulator** to model your research question
2. Build a population model in the **Population Health Modeler**
3. Run cost-effectiveness analysis in the **CEA Calculator**
4. Cross-reference findings with the **50-State Dashboard** for empirical data

### Vermont-Specific Research
HTR has the deepest available coverage of Vermont's health system:
- **Vermont Medicaid** (`/vermont-medicaid`) — complete program overview
- **Vermont Act 167** (`/vermont-act-167`) — 2022 healthcare transformation law
- **Vermont Act 68** (`/vermont-act-68`) — 2025 legislation
- **Vermont RHT Program** (`/vermont-rht-program`) — rural health policy
- **CMS AHEAD Model** (`/ahead-model`) — Vermont's global budget participation
- **Vermont Hospital Profiles** (`/dashboard/vermont/hospitals`) — facility-level data

### Comparative State Research
1. Use the **50-State Dashboard** (`/dashboard`) as your data foundation
2. Use **Dashboard > Compare** (`/dashboard/compare`) for side-by-side state comparison
3. Filter state data by Medicaid metrics, hospital capacity, health outcomes
4. Drill into individual hospital profiles for facility-level data

HTR Academy (`/academy`) has structured education aligned with research fields:

## Step 6: Saving and Organizing Research

- **Bookmarks:** Click the bookmark icon on any page to save to **My Library** (`/saved`)
- **My Library** organizes bookmarks by pillar — useful for literature review
- **Research Workspace** (`/research-lab/knowledge-workspace?tab=workspace`) — multi-tool research environment
- **AI Analyst history** persists within your session — use it to build a research thread
