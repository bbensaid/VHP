# Quick-Start Guide: Policy Analyst / Government Official
**Health Transformation Review (HTR) Platform**

---

## Your Role on HTR

As a policy analyst, Medicaid director, CMS staffer, or legislative analyst, HTR gives you:
- Structured analysis of federal and state health policy across all six pillars
- Simulation tools for modeling policy scenarios and Medicaid eligibility rules
- Comparative state data for benchmarking programs and proposals
- AI-powered research assistant trained on healthcare policy content

---

## Step 1: Set Your Role

At **/welcome**, click **"Policy Analyst / Government Official"**.

The AI Analyst will open with a prompt tailored to policy work — Medicaid waiver analysis, Vermont legislation, federal mandates, or a custom question.

---

## Step 2: Your 5 Most Valuable Destinations

### 1. Policy Hub — `/policy`
The core intelligence layer for policy work. Sub-sections:
- **Regulation & Legislation** (`/policy/regulation`) — federal rule analysis, CMS rulemaking
- **Public Health Mandates** (`/policy/mandates`) — state and federal mandates
- **Global & Comparative Policy** (`/policy/global`) — international benchmarks
- **Policy Feasibility Studies** (`/policy/feasibility`) — what's politically and operationally achievable

### 2. Policy Simulator — `/research-lab/policy-quality?tab=policy`
Model the downstream effects of proposed legislation or rule changes. Adjust parameters — coverage rates, reimbursement levels, provider participation — and see projected outcomes across utilization, cost, and equity metrics.

### 3. Vermont Program Analysis
HTR has deep coverage of Vermont-specific programs:
- **Vermont Act 167** (`/vermont-act-167`) — 2022 healthcare transformation law + scenario simulator
- **Vermont Act 68** (`/vermont-act-68`) — 2025 continuation + simulator
- **Vermont Medicaid** (`/vermont-medicaid`) — eligibility rules, waiver status
- **Vermont RHT Program** (`/vermont-rht-program`) — Rural Health Transformation

### 4. Medicaid Eligibility Simulator — `/medicaid-eligibility-simulator`
Model Vermont Medicaid eligibility under different income, household, and categorical scenarios. Essential for waiver analysis and constituent casework.

### 5. 50-State Dashboard — `/dashboard`
Compare state Medicaid programs, hospital landscapes, and health outcomes across all 50 states. Use **Dashboard > Compare** (`/dashboard/compare`) for side-by-side state analysis.

## Step 3: Key Workflows

### Analyzing a Proposed Federal Rule
1. Search for the rule at `/search` (use rule number, e.g., "CMS-1234")
2. Read the HTR analysis in **Policy > Regulation**
3. Ask the AI: *"What are the second-order effects of [rule] on critical access hospitals?"*
4. Run the **Policy Simulator** to model impact on your state

### Preparing a Legislative Brief
1. Use **AI Analyst** to draft a plain-language summary of the legislation
2. Pull comparative state data from the **50-State Dashboard**
3. Check **Policy Feasibility Studies** for precedents
4. Export key data points and save to **My Library** via bookmarks

### Tracking Vermont-Specific Programs
1. Bookmark `/vermont-act-167` and `/vermont-act-68` as your starting points
2. Use the Act 167 Simulator to model how scenario changes affect program outcomes
3. Check **The Wire** (`/the-wire`) filtered for Vermont news
4. Ask the AI: *"What is the current status of Vermont's AHEAD Model participation?"*

### State-to-State Benchmarking
1. Go to `/dashboard/compare`
2. Select Vermont and 2–3 comparison states
3. Compare Medicaid metrics, hospital capacity, program adoption
4. Export findings for your brief or report
