# Quick-Start Guide: Clinician (MD, NP, PA)
**Health Transformation Review (HTR) Platform**

---

## Your Role on HTR

As a clinician — physician, nurse practitioner, or physician assistant — HTR gives you:
- Clinical innovation intelligence across hospital-at-home, precision medicine, virtual care, and genomics
- Population health and health equity analysis tools
- AI-powered research for clinical policy questions
- Continuing education through the HTR Academy

---

## Step 1: Set Your Role

At **/welcome**, click **"Clinician (MD, NP, PA)"**.

The AI will open ready to help with hospital-at-home models, precision medicine, clinical quality, health equity, or any other clinical topic you raise.

---

## Step 2: Your 5 Most Valuable Destinations

### 1. Clinical Hub — `/clinical`
The primary intelligence layer for clinical content:
- **Hospital-at-Home** (`/clinical/hah`) — regulatory landscape, clinical protocols, CMS waiver status
- **Precision Medicine** (`/clinical/precision`) — genomics, biomarkers, targeted therapy
- **Virtual Care Models** (`/clinical/virtual`) — telehealth policy, reimbursement, outcomes
- **Genomics & Predictive Medicine** (`/clinical/genomics`) — emerging applications and policy
- **Population Health Management** (`/clinical/population`) — risk stratification, panel management

### 2. Health Equity Studio — `/research-lab/population-equity?tab=equity`
Model equity gaps across demographics and geographies. Explore SDOH drivers, access disparities, and algorithmic bias in clinical tools. Essential for quality improvement and DEI reporting.

### 3. Population Health Modeler — `/research-lab/population-equity?tab=population`
Build population health models: risk stratification, chronic disease burden, preventive care impact. Useful for panel management and APM quality metric planning.

### 4. Clinical Quality Optimizer — `/research-lab/policy-quality?tab=quality`
Optimize clinical quality metrics — HEDIS, CMS Star Ratings, VBC quality bonuses. Model the impact of protocol changes on your quality scores.

### 5. AI Analyst — `/chat`
Ask clinical policy questions in plain language:
- *"What does the current CMS Hospital-at-Home waiver cover?"*
- *"What are the evidence-based quality metrics for a diabetes management program?"*
- *"How does Vermont's Act 167 affect clinical quality reporting requirements?"*

## Step 3: Key Workflows

### Staying Current on Clinical Policy
1. Check **The Wire** (`/the-wire`) for clinical and regulatory news
2. Browse **Clinical hub** sub-sections for in-depth analysis
3. Ask the AI: *"Summarize what changed in CMS's hospital outpatient prospective payment system this year"*
4. Set alerts at `/connect/alerts` for clinical topics

### Preparing for a Value-Based Care Transition
1. Take the **VBC Readiness Assessment** (`/research-lab/knowledge-workspace?tab=readiness`)
2. Review your clinical quality metrics in the **Clinical Quality Optimizer**
3. Model population risk in the **Population Health Modeler**
4. Ask the AI for guidance on specific quality measures you need to hit

### Understanding Health Equity in Your Practice
1. Go to **Equity hub** (`/equity`)
2. Explore **SDOH** (`/equity/sdoh`), **Algorithmic Bias** (`/equity/bias`), and **Access Disparity** (`/equity/access`)
3. Use the **Health Equity Studio** to model gaps in your patient population
4. Check the **Bed Capacity & Transfer tool** (`/bed-capacity`) for regional access data

### Continuing Education
1. Visit **HTR Academy** (`/academy`)
2. Start with **Personalized Learning** (`/academy/personalized-learning`) — select Clinician role
3. Browse courses by pillar (Clinical, Equity, Policy)
4. Register for webinars (`/academy/webinars`) — expert-led clinical and policy sessions
