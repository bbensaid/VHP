# Quick-Start Guide: Hospital / Health System Executive
**Health Transformation Review (HTR) Platform**

---

## Your Role on HTR

As a Hospital or Health System Executive (CEO, CFO, COO, CMO), HTR gives you:
- Real-time intelligence on federal and state policy that affects your operations
- Financial modeling tools to stress-test your organization against VBC transitions
- AI-powered analysis you can query in plain language
- Benchmarking data across 50 states and thousands of hospitals

---

## Step 1: Set Your Role (30 seconds)

When you first visit the platform, you land on **/welcome**.

1. Click **"Hospital / Health System Executive"**
2. You're taken directly to the AI Analyst chat with a greeting tailored to your priorities
3. The AI will ask what you're solving: financial modeling, VBC readiness, workforce, regulatory compliance, or something else

> **Tip:** Your role is saved in your browser. You can change it anytime by going back to `/welcome`.

---

## Step 2: Your 5 Most Valuable Destinations

### 1. HTR Transformation Readiness Simulator — `/htr-simulator`
The flagship tool. Answer questions about your organization, and the simulator generates a readiness score across all six pillars. Use it to identify your biggest risk areas before a board meeting or strategic planning session.

### 2. 50-State Dashboard — `/dashboard`
Find any state's hospital landscape, payer mix, Medicaid metrics, and policy environment. Drill into individual hospital profiles. Use **Dashboard > Compare** to benchmark your state against peers.

### 3. Research Lab: Hospital Financial Stress Test — `/research-lab/policy-quality?tab=scorecard`
Model how proposed policy changes (CMS rules, global budget transitions, Medicaid cuts) would affect your hospital's finances. Adjust assumptions and see projected impact in real time.

### 4. AI Analyst — right sidebar or `/chat`
Ask anything in plain language:
- *"What does H.R. 1 mean for rural hospital reimbursement?"*
- *"Compare Vermont Act 167 and Act 68 for a critical access hospital."*
- *"What are the top 3 risks of moving to a global budget?"*

### 5. Economics Hub — `/economics`
Deep analysis on value-based care models, market and finance trends, labor strategy, and investment. Updated as major rules and market events occur.

## Step 3: Key Workflows

### Preparing for a Board Meeting
1. Run the **Transformation Readiness Simulator** — export your scorecard
2. Check the **50-State Dashboard** for your state's benchmark position
3. Ask the AI Analyst: *"Summarize the top 3 policy risks facing [your state] hospitals in the next 12 months"*
4. Browse **Operations > Workforce** (`/operations/workforce`) for current labor market data

### Evaluating a VBC Contract
1. Go to **Research Lab > APM Design Lab** (`/research-lab/payment-models?tab=apm-design`)
2. Model the contract structure — shared savings, downside risk, quality bonuses
3. Run the **Shared Savings Calculator** (`?tab=apm-calc`) to project financial impact
4. Ask the AI: *"What are the standard quality metrics in a CMS APM for a health system our size?"*

### Responding to a New CMS Rule
1. Check **The Wire** (`/the-wire`) for the latest news
2. Search the platform (`/search`) for the rule name or number
3. Read the **Policy hub** analysis (`/policy/regulation`)
4. Ask the AI for a plain-English summary and operational implications

## Step 4: Bookmark Your Key Tools

Click the **bookmark icon** on any page to save it to **My Library** (`/saved`). Build a personal dashboard of the tools and analyses you return to most.

## Step 5: Set Up Alerts

Go to `/connect/alerts` to subscribe to policy alerts in your areas of interest. Get notified when major rules drop, state program changes are enacted, or new research is published.
