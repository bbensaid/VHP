*** OBJECTIVE ***
Generate a single, high-fidelity, long-form research article as a RAW MINIFIED JSON OBJECT.
This object must strictly adhere to the 'policyAnalysis' Sanity schema.
Think of this as a peer-reviewed journal article combined with an executive intelligence brief —
not a blog post. Depth, specificity, named data, and expert-level argumentation are mandatory.

*** 1. CORE EXECUTION RULES (CRITICAL — DO NOT BREAK THESE) ***
- OUTPUT ONLY THE JSON. No markdown fences (no ```json), no intro sentence, no outro.
- CHARACTER SANITIZATION: Strictly NO raw control characters (\n, \t, \r inside strings).
  Use \u2014 for em dash, \u2019 for right single quote/apostrophe, \u201c/\u201d for curly quotes.
- ESCAPING: All internal double-quotes must be escaped as \". All backslashes as \\.
- MINIFICATION: The entire output must be a single, continuous line of text with no line breaks.

*** 2. REQUIRED FIELD VALUES ***
- "pillar"     : MUST be exactly one of: "Policy" | "Economics" | "Technology" | "Clinical" | "Equity"
- "category"   : MUST match the subcategory label provided in Section 5 (e.g. "Value-Based Care Models")
- "status"     : MUST be exactly one of: "Active" | "Proposed" | "In Committee"
- "impactLevel": MUST be exactly one of: "Critical" | "High" | "Medium"
- "publishedAt": Use the ISO timestamp provided in Section 5. If none given, use current date in format "2026-01-15T09:00:00Z"

*** 3. LENGTH & DEPTH REQUIREMENTS (NON-NEGOTIABLE) ***

MINIMUM BODY BLOCKS: 35 blocks. Target 40-50 blocks for a full-length article.
MINIMUM PROSE WORD COUNT: Each normal paragraph must contain AT LEAST 60 words.
  Do not write short, thin paragraphs. Every paragraph must advance the argument with
  specific data, named programs, dollar figures, named legislation, or expert-level analysis.
  A paragraph that could have been written without domain expertise is not acceptable.

REQUIRED CONTENT STRUCTURE — the article must contain ALL of the following:
  - 1  opening lead paragraph (normal) — sets the crisis, stakes, and scope. No heading before it.
  - 5-7 h2 section headings, each representing a major chapter of the analysis
  - 2-3 h3 sub-headings distributed across sections (never immediately after an h2)
  - 8-12 normal paragraphs of substantive prose — each 60+ words, data-rich
  - 2  quote blocks — high-impact statistics or authoritative assertions with source context
  - 2  callout blocks — one policy implication, one executive action item
  - 2  bullet lists — each with 4-6 items, each item a complete sentence (not a fragment)
  - 1  numbered list — a step-by-step process, ranked findings, or implementation sequence
  - 2  data tables — each with at least 4 rows and 3 columns of real, specific data
  - 1  image block — caption must be a detailed technical description (2-3 sentences)
  - 1  audio block — MANDATORY. Use a plausible HTR podcast URL and write a substantive summary.
  - 1  video block — MANDATORY. Use a plausible YouTube URL and write a substantive caption.

DEPTH STANDARDS — every article must include:
  - At least 6 specific dollar figures, percentages, or quantitative data points
  - At least 3 named federal programs, legislation, or regulatory rules (e.g. CONNECT Act, CMS AHEAD Model)
  - At least 2 named geographic examples (states, regions, or specific health systems)
  - At least 1 forward-looking projection or scenario analysis
  - A conclusion section (h2: "Executive Takeaway" or similar) with concrete next steps

*** 4. BODY COMPONENT RULES (THE "NEVER-FAIL" RULES) ***

CRITICAL: Every item in the "body" array MUST have a unique "_key" field.
Format: "_key": "blk[sequential_3digit_number]"  e.g. "blk001", "blk002", "blk003" ... "blk042"

HEADING HIERARCHY RULES (enforce strictly):
- Use h2 for MAJOR section breaks (5-7 per article). Think of these as chapter titles.
- Use h3 for sub-points WITHIN a section. An h3 must always appear after an h2, never before.
- NEVER place two h2 blocks back-to-back without at least two normal paragraphs between them.
- NEVER place an h3 immediately after an h2 — always write at least one normal paragraph first.
- The article must NOT open with a heading. The first block must always be a normal paragraph.
- Natural rhythm: h2 -> paragraph -> paragraph -> h3 -> paragraph -> paragraph -> quote -> h2...
