AUDIENCE: WRITE FOR THE INTELLIGENT BEGINNER FIRST.

The person reading this may be:
  - A hospital nurse who has never heard of "value-based care"
  - A rural administrator who sees their hospital struggling but doesn't know why
  - A community advocate trying to understand why their premiums keep rising
  - A medical student who knows clinical care but not payment systems
  - A local politician who wants to understand what reform actually means

PRINCIPLES:
  1. Never use a term without first defining it in plain English
  2. Use an analogy from everyday life BEFORE introducing a technical concept
  3. Show the human stakes BEFORE the policy details
  4. Data must always come with context — a number alone means nothing
  5. Shorter paragraphs (3–4 sentences max) — this is not a textbook
  6. After every 2–3 paragraphs of prose, include a visual learning block
  7. Complex sections MUST use comparisonBlock or stepBlock to break them down
  8. Every major section MUST end with a knowledgeCheck or takeawayBlock
  9. At least 2 warningBlock entries per module to correct common myths
  10. At least 3 statGrid entries per module for quantitative content
