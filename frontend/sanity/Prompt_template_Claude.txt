*** OBJECTIVE ***
Generate a single, high-fidelity research article as a RAW MINIFIED JSON OBJECT.
This object must strictly adhere to the 'policyAnalysis' Sanity schema.

*** 1. CORE EXECUTION RULES (CRITICAL — DO NOT BREAK THESE) ***
- OUTPUT ONLY THE JSON. No markdown fences (no ```json), no intro sentence, no outro.
- CHARACTER SANITIZATION: Strictly NO raw control characters (\n, \t, \r inside strings).
  Use \u2014 for em dash, \u2019 for right single quote/apostrophe, \u201c/\u201d for curly quotes.
- ESCAPING: All internal double-quotes must be escaped as \". All backslashes as \\.
- MINIFICATION: The entire output must be a single, continuous line of text with no line breaks.

*** 2. REQUIRED FIELD VALUES ***
- "pillar"     : MUST be exactly one of: "Policy" | "Economics" | "Technology" | "Clinical" | "Equity"
- "category"   : MUST match the subcategory label provided in Section 4 (e.g. "Value-Based Care Models")
- "status"     : MUST be exactly one of: "Active" | "Proposed" | "In Committee"
- "impactLevel": MUST be exactly one of: "Critical" | "High" | "Medium"
- "publishedAt": Use the ISO timestamp provided in Section 4. If none given, use current date in format "2026-01-15T09:00:00Z"

*** 3. BODY COMPONENT RULES (THE "NEVER-FAIL" RULES) ***

CRITICAL: Every item in the "body" array MUST have a unique "_key" field.
Format: "_key": "blk[sequential_3digit_number]"  e.g. "blk001", "blk002", "blk003"...

MINIMUM BODY REQUIREMENT: Generate AT LEAST 12 body blocks. A research article should include:
- 1 lead paragraph (normal) — no heading before it; drop the reader directly into the argument
- 2–4 h2 section headings, each followed by 2–3 normal paragraphs before the next h2
- 1–2 h3 sub-headings used WITHIN a section to break a complex argument into named parts
- 1 quote block (a key statistic or expert-level assertion)
- 1 callout block (a policy implication, alert, or action item)
- 1 data table (code block with language json)
- 1 image block (caption + alt only — NO asset or _ref field)
- Optional: 1 audio or video block if a briefing URL is provided

HEADING HIERARCHY RULES (enforce strictly):
- Use h2 for MAJOR section breaks only (2–4 per article). Think of these as chapter titles.
- Use h3 for sub-points WITHIN a section. An h3 must always appear after an h2, never before.
- NEVER place two h2 blocks back-to-back without at least one normal paragraph between them.
- NEVER place an h3 immediately after an h2 — always write at least one normal paragraph first.
- The article must NOT open with a heading. The first block must always be a normal paragraph.
- Aim for a natural reading rhythm: h2 → paragraph → paragraph → h3 → paragraph → quote/callout → h2...
