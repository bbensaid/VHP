# Chapter 16 and Appendices A–E — Proposed Changes

## Chapter 16 — keep the structure; fix, sharpen, connect to Chapter 15

Chapter 16 is the stronger of the two chapters: current, statute-based, and specific to AHS. It needs targeted changes, not a rewrite.

### C16-1 Figure 16.2, row 1 — factual error

**Now:** "…must work with separate AHS programs (DVHA, DMH, ADS, DAIL, Health Care Reform)…"

**Proposed:** "…must work with separate AHS departments and offices (DVHA, DMH, DAIL, DCF, the Office of Health Care Reform) — and, for anything involving technology, with the Agency of Digital Services, which is a separate agency…"

**Why:** ADS is not part of AHS. Under 3 V.S.A. §§ 3301–3302 it is a separate agency that approves all executive-branch IT activities and supervises all IT staff.

### C16-2 Figure 16.2 — add a fifth structural gap

| Gap | What it looks like in practice | Consequence | Required structural change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price regulation and transformation in separate bodies | GMCB orders each hospital's budget and prices; AHS shapes its transformation plan and RHT funding. The two are reviewed in different buildings, on different calendars. | A hospital that loses price chooses its own cuts; the system's redesign is decided by budget orders no one reads against the plans. | A joint annual AHS–GMCB review, hospital by hospital, of budget orders beside transformation plans (Chapter 15.9). |

### C16-3 Section 16.2.2 (Act 119) — add what the review actually found

**Add after the existing first paragraph:**

"The review's findings matter more than its structural conclusion. Participants asked for clearer decision-making authority and accountability across departments and leadership levels; described chronic staffing shortages, workload pressures, and limited professional development; called the agency's technology fragmented and outdated; and noted that in an agency of six departments, roughly 4,000 staff, and a multibillion-dollar budget, only a limited number of issues can reach the executive level at any time. Those are the conditions under which AHS must deliver Act 68."

*Source: AHS, Act 119 Report (November 1, 2025).*

### C16-4 Section 16.3.1 (Level 1, Statewide System Architect) — add two capacities

- **State-action supervision.** Act 68 (Sec. 11a) requires AHS to facilitate and supervise hospital collaboration so that it qualifies for state-action immunity under federal and state antitrust law. That is a legal-supervisory function — documented decisions, conditions, and monitoring — that a benefits agency has not had to perform before, and it needs a named owner.
- **Technology partnership.** AHS cannot approve its own IT projects, and since Act 62 of 2025 it holds the State's main leverage over the health information exchange through DVHA's HIT Plan and the VITL contract. The Statewide System Architect needs a standing, senior counterpart at ADS and a contract-management capability for VITL (Chapter 15.5).

### C16-5 Section 16.3.1 — name the statutory coordinator

**Add:** "Act 68 also amended 3 V.S.A. § 3027 to make the Director of Health Care Reform responsible for coordinating health care system reform across all executive-branch agencies, departments, and offices, and with the GMCB, and to report every February 15 using an evidence-based evaluation framework. The coordinating role this chapter describes therefore already exists in statute; what it lacks is staff and decision rights proportionate to the mandate."

### C16-6 Section 16.6 (Strategic Plan outline), item 6 — make Data and Technology concrete

**Replace the item with:** "Data and Technology Infrastructure — Technology (10–15 pp) — the § 9353 data-use decisions made by the HIE Steering Committee and the uses approved; Unified Health Data Space phases (Medicaid live January 2026; multi-payer 2028–2029; commercial 2029–2030) set against GMCB's FY2028 price and budget dates; exchange performance and portability terms in the VITL contract; MDWAS and VHCURES analytic capability; the statewide-EHR decision."

### C16-7 Section 16.6, item 9 — add decision rights

**Append:** "…and an AHS–ADS–GMCB–VITL decision-rights charter for every reform-critical system (the Vermont Delivery Compact, Chapter 15.7)."

### C16-8 Key Concepts — add

State-action supervision (Act 68 Sec. 11a) · Director of Health Care Reform (3 V.S.A. § 3027) · § 9353 data gate.
