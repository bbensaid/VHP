**My own earlier error, stated plainly:** earlier this session I wrote the §1.3 box's Simulator sentence after
checking the page's own marketing excerpt (`htr-simulator/page.tsx:266`, "this simulator enforces the dependency
logic Chapter 1 argues"). That text was a claim on the page, not behaviour. Rows 15, 20, 21, 22, 23 and 24 were
all one root cause: **no tool implemented the gate/penalty logic the chapter tells readers to watch** — while
three platform pages printed that it did.

**Resolved 2026-09-21 by building it, at the author's instruction** ("build that fucking behaviour"). The book's
wording stands; the platform now does what it says. The nine dependencies live in one place
(`frontend/lib/framework/dependencies.ts`), the logic in `sequence-engine.ts`, and three tools read them:

- `/htr-simulator` — per-pillar scoring, nominal vs delivered, every gate OPEN/PARTIAL/CLOSED, binding
  constraint by sensitivity, the Equity Imperative reported and never averaged in. Presets reproduce §1.2's
  OneCare profile (47 on paper → **24.3 delivered**) and §1.15's reordering (same Economics input, rescued).
- `/transformation-friction-index` — was a static explainer; now scores friction and names the binding
  constraint, the Technology gate by default.
- `/impact-simulation` — 36-month propagation: a Technology shortfall hits Clinical at month 9, **Economics at
  month 12** (§1.15's own figure), Operations at month 15.

Verified: 23 engine unit tests (`npm run test:engine`) asserting the book's claims, 5 browser tests
(`e2e/framework-tools.spec.ts`), full e2e suite 23/23 green twice, `tsc --noEmit` clean, production build
clean, both doctrine guards clean. Model assumptions (gate thresholds, penalty curve, edge lags other than the
book's twelve months) are labelled as such in the UI.
