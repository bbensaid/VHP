387	Start with the pressure points and the no-slack components from the first three steps: each is a risk to everything that depends on it.
388	Then read the register as Lesson 11 does: plot it, group risks by shared cause, and check which can be reduced by work that is unblocked today.
389	Build the benefits trace from Lesson 12.
390	It is tempting to leave the most uncomfortable risks off a register you will show to others, especially risks about leadership, capacity or political will.
391	Chapter 15's own register puts the absence of the portfolio function at the top.
392	An honest register is the only useful one.
