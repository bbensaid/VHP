## Act 68 of 2025 (signed June 12, 2025)
- RBP: GMCB must begin "as soon as practicable but not later than hospital fiscal year 2027" (18 V.S.A. § 9375/9456).
  GMCB Feb 17, 2026 Act 68 update: methodology set by rule in 2027, maximum rates effective HFY2028.
- Global budgets: "Beginning not later than HFY2028, to the extent that resources are available ... one or more Vermont
  hospitals that are not critical access hospitals"; all hospitals by HFY2030, also "to the extent resources are
  available". Global budgets "shall include Medicare to the extent permitted under federal law but shall NOT include
  Medicaid". (Legislative Counsel's summary wrongly says FY2027 for non-CAH.)
- AHS must coordinate ≥2.5% reduction in hospital spending for HFY2026; monthly updates Oct 1, 2025–Sep 30, 2026.
- AHS outcome-measure reports monthly Aug 1, 2025 – Jan 1, 2027.
- Statewide Health Care Delivery Strategic Plan due Jan 15, 2028 (§ 9403(d)(3)); updates every 3 years from
  Dec 1, 2030. Plan must include annual TCOC targets and targets for % of spending on primary care (incl. mental
  health) and preventive care (§ 9403(b)(7)).
- HCAC (§ 9403a): 18 members, chaired by AHS Secretary; GMCB Chair, Chief Health Care Advocate, 2 primary-care
  steering committee members, 1 commercial insurer, 2 hospitals, FQHC, physicians (VMS), independent practices
  (HealthFirst), APRNs, designated agencies, preferred provider, independent MH clinician, home health, LTC, small
  business. NO pharmacy seat. Sets affordability benchmarks; advises legislature; consults Health Equity Advisory Comm.
- Also creates 16-member Vermont Steering Committee for Comprehensive Primary Health Care.
- GMCB may share subpoenaed material with AHS/DFR; providers must give unredacted contracts on request; GMCB+DFR must
  monitor whether RBP price cuts reach premiums. 3 new GMCB positions, all for RBP. $2M AHS incentive grants.
- Unified Health Data Space: AHS with HIE Steering Committee; clinical–claims integration not before Jan 1, 2027,
  only on a Steering Committee majority vote.
- Act 68 does NOT order AHS to reorganize (that is Oliver Wyman's recommendation).
- S.190 (2026, faster RBP) VETOED June 16, 2026.
- Act 119 of 2024: AHS re-envisioning review; Feb 4, 2026 report: keep AHS unified ("feedback consistently favored
  better coordination over restructuring"); move clinical substance-use functions into DMH.

## Federal / AHEAD / Medicaid
- Vermont signed AHEAD state agreement Jan 2025; withdrew July 2026 after CMS renegotiation cut the expected federal
  upside (EAST Fund) from ~$138M to a cap of $10M. Medicare's primary-care $ ended with OneCare; RHT = stopgap.
- Medicaid hospital global budget (6 hospitals, launched Jan 1, 2026): CMS letter July 7, 2026 — not approved under
  Global Commitment STCs; payments stopped July 31, 2026; Jan–Jul payments reprocessed as FFS by Jan 7, 2027
  (GCR 26-089). Global Commitment waiver expires Dec 31, 2027.
- Vermont All-Payer ACO Model: 2018–2022, extended 2023, transition year 2024 (concluded 2024).
- RHT: Vermont FY2026 award $195,053,740 (Dec 29, 2025); later years set annually through FY2030. RHT application
  activity list: no tele-ICU, no neurology/oncology tele-consult line, no broadband funding; voluntary shared EMR
  (program-manager solicitation RHT_AHS_004_FY26, May 20, 2026); e-Consult; RPM ($0.9M yr1); AI scribes ($1.03M yr1).
- CCBHC: 4 certified July 1, 2026 (NKHS, Howard Center, NCSS, HCRS) + Clara Martin and Rutland MH earlier = 6.
- Medicaid work requirements begin Jan 1, 2027 (H.R. 1).

## Oliver Wyman Act 167 report (presented Sept 18, 2024; cover Aug 2024; revised Oct 21, 2024; updated file Jan 31, 2025)
- 9 of 14 hospitals operating losses in 2023 (worst −8.9%); 13 of 14 in loss by 2028.
- 5-yr deficit $0.7BN (3.5% revenue / 5% expense growth) or $2.4BN (7–8% expense growth). ">$400MM direct savings in
  5 years", independent of payment-reform savings.
- Silver premium $456 (2018) → $948 (2024) +108% (exec summary also says $985); income $60,781→$73,991 (+22%).
- 2020 age mix: 0–19 21.7%, 20–64 58.8% (367K), 65+ ≈19.4%; 2040: 20–64 318K (−13%), 65+ ≈31%.
- Three imperatives: build housing & fix transportation; pay PPS hospitals with RBP and move to global
  budgets/capitation when conditions are met; move all care possible out of hospitals. RBP "ideally at 200% of
  Medicare or less for PPS".
- At-risk hospitals: Grace Cottage, Gifford, North Country, Springfield. REH "likely not supportable in the area"
  (worst case) for NCH/Gifford/Springfield; not in Grace Cottage final recs; Mt Ascutney "may be difficult".
- COEs: UVMMC 16, SVMC 9, BMH 6, CVMC 5, RRMC 5, NMC 4, NVRH 4, Springfield 2, Copley 2, Mt Ascutney 1; Grace
  Cottage/Gifford/NCH/Porter need further discussion. "10 OUT OF 13 CURRENT HSAS".
- "Add Division of Planning and Effectiveness" is a GMCB action ("assist AHS in calculations"); AHS actions: align
  AHS agencies with HSAs, fully computerize/integrate subunits, review structure; PMO support.
- Engagement: 230+ meetings, 3,100+ participants, 100+ organizations.

## Vermont data
- HSAs: Vermont Department of Health counts 13 (Townshend inside Brattleboro); 14 hospitals. Pop July 1, 2025:
  644,663. Use 13; describe OW engagement as "across all of Vermont's hospital service areas".
- Personal health care provider (VDH BRFSS 2023–2024, March 2026): VT 91, White 91, Hispanic 78 (only group
  statistically below VT), Black 85, AIAN 82. AHS benchmark 87%. Never "11-point BIPOC gap".
- AHS Nov 2025 report: 30-day readmission 14.8%; potentially avoidable ED 32.3% (Oliver Wyman 2020–2022
  VUHDDS/VHCURES); 30-day follow-up after ED visit for mental illness 81% (PCMH-attributed); suicide/self-harm ED
  245.5 per 10,000, higher in Rutland & Windham; CAH op profit $938 vs $2,784; UVMMC admin $3,826 vs $1,427.
- GMCB: commercial payers ~250–300% of Medicare (avg ~289%); outpatient 279–697% by hospital (2024 claims); outpatient
  imaging up to 715–944%; breakeven ~136%. FY27 commercial rates −2.9% statewide (GMCB 9/15/2026).
- RAND: 2020 study UVMMC 358% (highest in VT); Aug 2024 round: VT systems 148–321%, overall 283%, outpatient 311%.
- UVMMC FY23 overage $80.3M, RRMC $11.1M; settlement (April 2025) amended the order to spread remaining reductions
  over FY26–FY27, contingent on UVMMC dropping appeals. No court ruling. "First enforcement ever" is UNCONFIRMED.
- VHCURES: quarterly collection, ~6-month claims run-out; "two years" = age of AHS published measures. MDWAS live
  early Jan 2026. VITL = clinical HIE only (does not carry VHCURES claims). Blueprint practice profiles = VHCURES,
  summary-level.
- Census: 11 states with more 65+ than children in 2024; Maine oldest (44.8). Chartis 2026: 41.2% rural hospitals in
  red. CHQPR 2026: 700+ at risk, 294 immediate.
- Act 167 goals (5): reduce inefficiencies, lower costs, improve health outcomes, reduce health inequities, increase
  access to essential services.
