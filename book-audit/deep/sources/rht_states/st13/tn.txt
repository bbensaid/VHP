Tennessee Announces $422 Million in Rural Health Transformation Grants for 138 Projects Statewide Skip to Main Content Tennessee Announces $422 Million in Rural Health Transformation Grants for 138 Projects Statewide Monday, November 09, 2026 | 02:07pm NASHVILLE, Tenn. — The Tennessee Department of Health is announcing the full list of Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) awards supporting 138 grantee projects to expand access to care and connect rural Tennesseans to better care closer to home. “Tennessee is leading the way in transforming rural health care and ensuring families can access the services they need closer to home,” Tennessee Gov. Bill Lee said. “These locally-driven solutions will improve maternal and child health, expand access to care, and strengthen health outcomes in rural communities across our state. Through the Rural Health Transformation Program, we are building on Tennessee’s commitment to helping every Tennessean live a healthy and productive life, no matter where they live.” RHTP funding to the 138 selected grantees will represent more than $422 million of total investment in rural health initiatives in Tennessee over the next five years of the grant program’s lifecycle. A detailed spreadsheet of the grant recipients, individual projects selected, and awarded project amounts is available on this page of the RHTP website . The nationwide program represents a five-year commitment of $50 billion to improve access to care, quality and health outcomes in rural communities across the country. “The Trump Administration’s Rural Health Transformation Program has a simple formula for success: state innovation backed by federal funds equals lasting improvements for rural communities," CMS Administrator Dr. Mehmet Oz said. "Investing in locally driven solutions is an important step toward achieving that vision for rural Tennessee and CMS is excited to partner with states to build stronger, more resilient rural health systems."
