The program focuses on helping people get the care they need where they live, leading to a healthier, thriving state. RHTP funding will be distributed across six initiatives, 29 CMS-approved programs. The tables on the following pages show both expenditures and obligated funds. Obligations are required by CMS to secure federal dollars within the budget period and reflect the agency’s commitment to moving each program into implementation. Website Refresh Launched a refreshed RHTP website with improved navigation and increased transparency, including the posting of numerous funding awards and program information.
