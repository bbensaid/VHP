This investment will expand healthcare training opportunities and help rural providers recruit and retain the professionals they need. This transformational funding will deliver $37.7 million to build Louisiana’s rural healthcare workforce through education, training, recruitment, and retention initiatives, including up to $20 million to connect Louisiana’s public universities and community and technical colleges with rural healthcare providers, aligning education and training with local workforce needs. The funding also includes $12.7 million to help 53 rural providers recruit and retain physicians, nurses, dentists, EMS personnel, behavioral health professionals, and other clinicians through incentives, including sign-on and retention bonuses, relocation assistance, and professional training. The additional $5 million will create healthcare career pathways across 39 campuses, helping high school students begin healthcare coursework, preparing participants to enter the healthcare workforce, and helping current healthcare workers advance their credentials while staying on the job. The Rural Health Transformation Program is expanding access to quality healthcare, regardless of a patient’s zip code, and equipping first responders with the lifesaving resources they need when every second counts. Today’s announcement is one part of the larger overall funding amount awarded to Louisiana for fiscal year 2026. Key initiatives the overall RHTP funding will focus on include: Building the rural healthcare workforce: Supporting recruitment, training, and retention strategies to strengthen the pipeline of healthcare professionals serving rural communities. Strengthening rural facilities and access: Supporting partnerships, operational improvements, and care-delivery changes that help rural providers maintain essential services and improve access. Advancing innovative care and payment models: Supporting new approaches to delivery and financing designed to improve quality, strengthen primary care, and support sustainable rural healthcare. Expanding community-based and preventive care: Investing in school-based care, mobile services, and other community strategies that improve prevention, address chronic disease, and expand access to care. Modernizing rural healthcare technology: Expanding remote care and data-driven tools to improve coordination and extend healthcare services to rural communities. The Trump Administration is delivering on its commitment to strengthen rural America by investing in solutions that modernize technology to promote health and well-being for rural communities. Led by CMS, the RHTP is moving beyond the failed one-size-fits-all approach and giving rural communities the tools to build stronger, more innovative, and more sustainable health systems that deliver better care and better outcomes for the Americans they serve.
