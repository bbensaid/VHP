This investment will support clinician recruitment and training, expand mobile and telehealth services, and modernize rural hospitals and clinics to help more Georgians receive care in their communities. Georgia has committed its full $219 million in funding to improve healthcare in rural communities across the state. This transformational $219 million investment supports five coordinated initiatives addressing healthcare access across Georgia’s 126 rural and partially rural counties. Investments will deploy mobile clinics and telehealth pods; strengthen maternal, pediatric, behavioral, and preventive care; and support training, residencies, and incentives to attract and retain rural clinicians. This funding will also help rural hospitals improve electronic medical records, cybersecurity, and other technologies that connect providers and strengthen patient care. The Rural Health Transformation Program is expanding access to quality healthcare, regardless of a patient’s ZIP code, while equipping first responders with the lifesaving resources they need when every second counts. Today’s announcement highlights the overall funding amount awarded to Georgia for fiscal year 2026. Key initiatives the overall RHTP funding will focus on include: Building the rural healthcare workforce: Supporting recruitment, training, and retention strategies to strengthen the pipeline of healthcare professionals serving rural communities. Strengthening rural facilities and access: Supporting partnerships, operational improvements, and care-delivery changes that help rural providers maintain essential services and improve access. Advancing innovative care and payment models: Supporting new approaches to delivery and financing designed to improve quality, strengthen primary care, and support sustainable rural healthcare. Expanding community-based and preventive care: Investing in school-based care, mobile services, and other community strategies that improve prevention, address chronic disease, and expand access to care. Modernizing rural healthcare technology: Expanding remote care and data-driven tools to improve coordination and extend healthcare services to rural communities. The Trump Administration is delivering on its commitment to strengthen rural America by investing in solutions that modernize technology to promote health and well-being for rural communities. Led by CMS, the RHTP is moving beyond the failed one-size-fits-all approach and giving rural communities the tools to build stronger, more innovative, and more sustainable health systems that deliver better care and better outcomes for the Americans they serve.
