This funding will support North Carolina’s Rural Health Innovation Fund, making funding available to eligible providers for technology upgrades and other digital healthcare advancements. The funding will help rural providers replace outdated technology and adopt modern tools that make it easier to deliver care and operate their practices. Eligible projects include expanding telehealth so patients can connect with care remotely, upgrading electronic health records, strengthening cybersecurity protections for health information, and adopting technology that helps clinicians make informed care decisions for their patients. This investment builds on the Administration’s $11.25 million in RHTP funding previously announced to expand preventive and behavioral healthcare through school-based health centers and strengthen North Carolina’s rural EMS workforce. “President Trump understands that every American deserves high-quality healthcare, no matter where they live,” said Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy, Jr. “We are putting resources directly into rural communities to strengthen the health workforce, modernize infrastructure, expand access, and bring care closer to home. We are giving states the flexibility to build solutions around the people they serve, while demanding accountability for results. That is how we strengthen rural health, improve the lives of patients and families, and Make America Healthy Again.” “Every state knows best what its rural communities need, and thanks to President Trump’s Rural Health Transformation Program, they now have the resources to meet those needs,” said CMS Administrator Dr. Mehmet Oz. “As states put these resources to work, we’re focused on turning these investments into meaningful, sustainable improvements for the health systems that serve rural North Carolinians.” Today’s investment was made possible through the Working Families Tax Cuts Act — passed by Republicans in Congress and unanimously opposed by Democrats — delivering a historic $50 billion investment to help states transform the way healthcare is delivered in America. “Meeting the health needs of North Carolina's rural communities requires the deployment of new technology and strategies that will help patients get care quicker and more conveniently. These investments under the Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) will help North Carolina providers improve both the quality and the availability of care to ensure our small communities are not left behind,” said Sen. Ted Budd. "Technology has enormous potential to improve how we deliver healthcare, particularly in rural communities where access to specialists and other medical services can be limited," said Rep. Greg Murphy. "Expanding telehealth, improving electronic health records, strengthening cybersecurity, and responsibly utilizing artificial intelligence can help rural providers deliver better and more efficient care to their patients. As a physician, I believe technology should strengthen the physician-patient relationship, not replace it. These investments will help rural providers modernize their systems while ensuring patients across North Carolina can receive high-quality care closer to home." “Access to quality healthcare should not depend on your ZIP code,” said Rep. Addison McDowell. “This $20 million investment will help break down barriers to telehealth, modernize the systems behind patient care, and give rural providers the tools they need to better serve their communities. Every American deserves access to high-quality care no matter where they live, and this program is bringing resources directly to the communities that need them most. Tim Moore. “This $20 million investment will help bring telehealth and new technologies to rural communities. Key initiatives the overall RHTP funding will focus on include: Connecting rural communities and expanding access: Supporting locally governed care networks that connect medical, behavioral health, and community services while strengthening access to primary and preventive care. Building the rural health workforce: Supporting training, recruitment, retention, and innovative care teams, including programs for doulas, community health workers, and other providers serving rural communities. Supporting sustainable rural providers: Helping rural hospitals and clinics strengthen operations and adopt sustainable approaches to care delivery and payment. Modernizing rural healthcare: Investing in telehealth, digital tools, health information technology, and other solutions that help providers improve care and better connect with patients. The Trump Administration is delivering on its commitment to strengthen rural America by investing in solutions that modernize, transform, and expand healthcare access for rural communities nationwide. Led by CMS, the RHTP is moving beyond the failed one-size-fits-all approach and giving rural communities the tools to build stronger, more innovative, and more sustainable health systems that deliver better care and better outcomes for the Americans they serve.
