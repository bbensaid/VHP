This investment will help build regional capacity and expand options for rural Floridians to receive high-quality healthcare closer to home. This transformational funding will expand options for rural Floridians to receive preventive, specialty, and follow-up care in their communities. Florida’s approach uses telemedicine, remote patient monitoring, pharmacy-based services, diagnostic kiosks, and community paramedicine to give patients more ways to access needed care. Partnerships are a defining feature of Florida’s approach, with more than 500 partnerships across the 45 awardees bringing hospitals, clinics, local governments, and community organizations together to support statewide rural healthcare transformation. The Rural Health Transformation Program is expanding access to quality healthcare, regardless of a patient’s ZIP code, while equipping first responders with the lifesaving resources they need when every second counts. Today’s announcement is one part of the larger overall funding amount awarded to Florida for fiscal year 2026. Key initiatives the overall RHTP funding will focus on include: Building the rural healthcare workforce: Supporting recruitment, training, and retention strategies to strengthen the pipeline of healthcare professionals serving rural communities. Strengthening rural facilities and access: Supporting partnerships, operational improvements, and care-delivery changes that help rural providers maintain essential services and improve access. Advancing innovative care and payment models: Supporting new approaches to delivery and financing designed to improve quality, strengthen primary care, and support sustainable rural healthcare. Expanding community-based and preventive care: Investing in school-based care, mobile services,and other community strategies that improve prevention, address chronic disease, and expand access to care. Modernizing rural healthcare technology: Expanding remote care and data-driven tools to improve coordination and extend healthcare services to rural communities. The Trump Administration is delivering on its commitment to strengthen rural America by investing in solutions that modernize technology to promote health and well-being for rural communities. Led by CMS, the RHTP is moving beyond the failed one-size-fits-all approach and giving rural communities the tools to build stronger, more innovative, and more sustainable health systems that deliver better care and better outcomes for the Americans they serve.
