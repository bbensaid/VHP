Gov. Braun Awards Grant to Transform Rural Healthcare 
 in Northeast Indiana Region 
 
Indianapolis, IN- Gov. Mike Braun announced today that the northeast Indiana region 
(Region 2) has been awarded $20.7 million in Growing Rural Opportunities for Well-
Being (GROW) initiative grants. GROW region two includes Adams, Cass, Dekalb, 
Huntington, Kosciusko, LaGrange, Miami, Noble, Steuben, Wabash, Wells and Whitley 
counties. 
 
GROW is focused on improving healthcare access, quality and outcomes in rural 
communities across the state of Indiana and supported by the Indiana Rural Health 
Transformation Program (RHTP), administered by the Indiana Department of Health 
(IDOH) and Family and Social Services Administration (FSSA). Indiana received nearly 
$207 million for the first of the five-year federal Centers for Medicare and 
Medicaid Services (CMS) grant program. About $120 million, or 60% of that funding, 
was earmarked for the regional grants.  
 
“The Trump Administration’s Rural Health Transformation Program is based on one 
simple idea: your access to quality health care shouldn’t depend on your ZIP code,” said 
CMS Administrator Dr. Mehmet Oz. “Funding new approaches to expand access and 
improve health outcomes for rural Hoosiers is an important step toward achieving that 
vision and I look forward to seeing RHTP dollars support equally transformative 
innovations for rural communities nationwide."  
 
“This funding is a major step forward in our commitment to making health care 
affordable and accessible for all Hoosiers,” Gov. Braun said. “It’s an important stride 
toward creating a more sustainable and connected network in rural Indiana.”    
 
Region 2 GROW grant activities include:   
 
• Coordinate a regional care network: Uses Project ECHO, RPM, Cardiometabolic 
Clinics, specialty referral pathways and community-based wellness programs to 
create greater access to care.   
 
 
  
   
 
 
• Reduce barriers to care: Expand same day primary care access, mobile health 
units, mobile diagnostics, telehealth, navigation services and behavioral health 
crisis response to address transportation, provider shortages, and long travel 
times.  
• Improve healthcare workforce: Strengthen clinical pipelines, expand rural 
training pathways, recruiting critical providers, implement telehealth-supported 
care models and integrate wraparound supports such as childcare and 
transportation for workforce stability.   
• Modernize and expand rural transportation systems; Coordinate regional 
planning, upgrade technology, stabilize workforce, expand vehicle availability and 
improve cross-county collaboration to enhance access to 
healthcare, employment and social resources.   
• Establishing County and Regional Coalition Coordinators: Unify hospitals, 
public health, EMS, behavioral health, transportation and community 
organizations under a common rural health strategy through shared 
implementation structures and cross-sector partnerships.   
 
Rural Health Transformation Program’s GROW will now move into implementation building on a 
foundation of strong collaboration, local leadership and meaningful impact from targeted 
activities.  
 
Region 2’s award complements the seven other regions that received GROW regional grants. 
RHTP also includes 11 statewide initiatives that promote workforce investment, thoughtful 
partnerships and infrastructure development. Additional information about the GROW regional 
grants and 11 statewide initiatives are available on the RHTP website growruralhealth.in.gov.
