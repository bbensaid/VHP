Welcome to Missouri's website for its Rural Health Transformation Program 

Missouri is participating in the Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP), a major five‑year federal initiative to strengthen healthcare in rural communities. Through the MO HealthNet Division, DSS is leading the effort with support from DHSS and DMH. Missouri has received more than $216 million in first‑year funding from CMS to begin building a sustainable, high‑quality rural healthcare system that will benefit Missourians for years to come. To learn more, please explore additional details on our website.

How Missouri is Transforming Rural Health

Our Vision

Every rural Missourian has access to the high-quality care they need through a delivery system that is well aligned, community anchored, and built to last.

Our Objectives

Improving health outcomes

Strengthen healthcare quality through integrated care coordination, aligned incentives, and evidence-based practices so that rural Missourians consistently experience seamless, high-value care.

Expanding access to care

Ensure rural Missourians can access primary and behavioral health providers close to home, community-based maternity options, with connections to specialists and complex care enabled by telehealth and provider interoperability. 

Strengthening provider sustainability

Reinforce the long-term sustainability of rural providers through targeted investments in infrastructure, adoption of innovative technologies, and payment models that reflect the realities of rural care delivery.

RHTP Strategic Pillars

  1. Regional Coordinating Networks and Hub Activation: Create local and regional groups that help providers coordinate care and focus on critical needs, spanning 104 counties
    RCNs will align and support 27 local community Hubs through consistent statewide coordination and execution, strengthening access to care and improving community health outcomes
     
  2. Alternative Payment Models: Use new payment approaches that reward teamwork and better health outcomes. 
    APMs will establish a shared-value payment model that reinvests savings from reduced avoidable cost and utilization back into rural communities
     
  3. Digital Backbone: Build shared technology systems so providers can communicate and share information easily. 
    The Digital Backbone will establish the technology foundation for ToRCH Care through platform interoperability, data modernization, and closed-loop social care referrals that enable coordinated care delivery
     
  4. Rural Health Workforce Programs: Train, recruit, and keep health care workers in rural areas.
    Workforce programs will strengthen the rural talent pipeline by cultivating, recruiting, training, and retaining clinicians and other critical health care professionals
     
  5. Provider Transformation: Help rural hospitals and clinics modernize how they operate, including strategic renovations
    Provider transformation will advance operational innovation and strategic facility investments that strengthen long-term sustainability while preserving access to care

Funding & Program Timeline

 For each budget period, recipients will have until the end of the following fiscal year October 30 to spend awarded funding. Budget Period 1 will start on December 29, 2025, and Subsequent Budget Periods will start on October 31 of each budget year.
