Gov. Rhoden awards $31.5 million in rural health grants

By Tamara Chibe
Published: Jul. 23, 2026 at 10:00 PM CDT
Add Us On GoogleAdd as a preferred source on Google

RAPID CITY, S.D. (KOTA) - Gov. Larry Rhoden has awarded $31.5 million in Rural Strong grants to 28 projects through South Dakota’s Rural Health Transformation Program.

The grants, selected from 79 eligible applications, will support projects across 20 health systems aimed at improving access to healthcare, modernizing technology, strengthening the rural workforce and expanding care coordination.

State officials said the funded projects also include initiatives focused on maternal health, Tribal communities and other priority populations.

Grant contracts will be posted publicly through OpenSD once finalized. The state also encouraged organizations that were not selected to pursue partnerships and future funding opportunities.

The Rural Health Transformation Program is designed to improve access to care and strengthen the long-term sustainability of rural healthcare across South Dakota.
