                                                                                                                                                                                                                      Year One Award List


  On Sept. 15, 2026, the South Carolina Department of Health and Human Services (SCDHHS) awarded contracts through the Rural Health Transformation
  Program to support projects that improve access to care, strengthen local health systems and advance innovative health care solutions in rural communities
  across South Carolina. The following organizations were awarded grant funding under the program. To date, all grant funds have been disbursed, and grantees
  are implementing projects in accordance with proposals that were evaluated by a committee through the standard notice of funding opportunity process. To
  ensure accountability and compliance with federal funding requirements, SCDHHS has contracted with a vendor to assist with program monitoring  and
  oversight in accordance with 2 CFR Part 200. Additionally, grantees are required to submit monthly progress reports, annual reports containing deidentified
  client-level  data, monthly  invoices  and  annual  fiscal  reports to support ongoing monitoring, evaluation and  fiscal accountability.
