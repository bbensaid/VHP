Alaska Department of Health Announces First Group of Rural Health Transformation 
Program Awards 
Rolling weekly announcements recognize projects that will strengthen access to care 
across rural Alaska 
ANCHORAGE, Alaska — August 7, 2026 — The Alaska Department of Health (DOH) today 
announced the first projects selected to receive Notices of Intent to Award through the 
Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP), marking the beginning of a series of funding 
announcements designed to strengthen access to health care in communities across 
Alaska. Additional notices will be announced each Friday as they are finalized, rather than 
waiting until the entire review process is complete. 
This first release includes intent to award over $4.5 million to 19 projects in communities 
across Alaska. A list of the projects is available here.   
These projects are designed to expand access to care, strengthen the health workforce, 
improve infrastructure, advance technology, and support innovative approaches that help 
rural and hub communities and regions meet their unique health care needs.  
Advocacy and support from Alaska's Congressional Delegation helped secure this historic 
investment in rural health care. 
"These awards represent more than individual projects, they represent a long-term 
investment in healthier communities across Alaska," said Commissioner Heidi Hedberg. 
"Throughout this process, we've heard directly from communities about the challenges 
they face and the solutions they believe will make the greatest difference. The selected   
projects reflect those local priorities while helping build a stronger, more sustainable 
health care system for future generations." 
“The Trump Administration’s Rural Health Transformation Program is helping states like 
Alaska develop innovative, locally driven solutions to improve rural Americans’ access to 
high-quality care," said CMS Administrator Dr. Mehmet Oz. "These first grant awards 

   
 
   
 
demonstrate how this historic investment is empowering rural communities to strengthen 
their health systems, put patients first, and bring better care closer to home.” 
Projects are selected based on criteria including alignment with Alaska’s RHTP goals, 
transformational potential, rural impact, sustainability, and contribution to a balanced 
statewide portfolio. Final award amounts and project terms remain subject to completion 
of budget review, resolution of any award conditions, and execution of a grant agreement. 
The Department is working directly with applicants and CMS to resolve outstanding 
questions and complete decisions as quickly as possible. 
The Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) is part of a $50 billion, five-year national 
initiative authorized by Congress supported by Alaska’s congressional delegation. The 
Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) awarded Alaska $272,174,856 for year 
one, one of the largest allocations in the nation.  
DOH is committed to responsible stewardship of federal funding. As additional awards are 
announced over the coming weeks, the department will continue updating the RHTP 
website with award information and related resources. Once all awards have been 
announced, DOH intends to publish a comprehensive public dashboard that allows 
Alaskans to explore the full portfolio of investments from multiple perspectives. 
For more information about the Rural Health Transformation Program, including weekly 
award announcements and program updates, visit the RHTP website.
