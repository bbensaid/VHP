Pilot test an ambulance service treatment in 
place project. The OEMS will define 
treatment in place and using this definition 
provide funds to rural ambulance services 
that opt into the project. Treatment in place 
is treatment that occurs when an ambulance 
service responds to a 911 call but does not 
transport the caller to the hospital. These 
calls are not reimbursable by insurance. 
OEMS will further refine this model and 
under the direction of MDH, implement this 
pilot model to test the impact of these funds 
for sustainability of rural ambulance services.
