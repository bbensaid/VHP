VIRGINIA INVESTS $122 MILLION IN RURAL HEALTH 
The investment, through the Rural Health Transformation Fund, will 
support rural providers, expand access to care, and invest in innovative 
technology 
 
RICHMOND, VA – Virginia has invested more than $122 million to support healthcare in rural 
communities through the Rural Health Transformation Program. The funding – a portion of the 
total $189 million in the Rural Health Transformation Fund for 2027 – will provide resources to 
rural healthcare providers, expand access to care across the Commonwealth, invest in 
innovative technology, and strengthen the rural healthcare workforce. 
 
“The ground is shifting under our health care system, and rural communities are feeling that 
uncertainty in real time,” said Secretary of Health and Human Resources Marvin B. Figueroa. 
“These investments allow us to create stability where we can, innovate where it makes sense, 
and keep care within reach for the people who need it.” 
 
The Rural Health Transformation Program serves more than 1.5 million Virginians across 76 
rural localities. This initial investment of $122 million is the first step in a multi-pronged 
process, with funding going to key partners that will then issue smaller, competitive grants to 
organizations working to strengthen healthcare access within rural communities, including 
health systems, clinics, educational institutions, and community-based organizations. The grant 
application process for eligible community organizations is now open. Those interested can 
click HERE for information about eligibility, deadlines, and more.  
 
“The future of rural health in Virginia is deeply personal — not just to me, but to the families 
and generations who call these communities home,” said Heidi Hertz, Director of Rural Health 
Transformation. “These funds strengthen the pipeline of providers committed to rural 
communities, expand access through technology, and reflect a bipartisan commitment to 
improving health and opportunity across rural Virginia.” 
 
The Commonwealth of Virginia is working with the following partners to issue grants to 
community-based organizations: 
 
• VA Hospital and Healthcare Association Foundation to support local organizations that 
can grow remote patient monitoring and rural residency slots, with a focus on high 
demand areas, while increasing access to healthy food and wellness resources. 
• VA Healthcare Foundation to support organizations advancing rural provider 
productivity and interoperability. 
• VA Foundation for Community College Education to grow the health workforce by 
supporting organizations engaging students in rural areas in allied health careers, like 
technicians and therapists or technologists. 
• VA Innovation Partnership Corporation to establish a technology innovation fund to 
help early-stage Virginia health technology companies scale digital health solutions. 
• VA Works to expand Earn to Learn programs and build on apprenticeship programs. 
• VA Department of Education to create a workforce pipeline through expanded rural 
healthcare credential programs. 
• VA Department of Health to invest in maternal care and community paramedicine. 
• VA Foundation for Healthy Youth to support organizations promoting active kids and 
consumer tech. 
• VA Department for Aging and Rehabilitative Services to better integrate care for rural 
Virginians dual-enrolled in both Medicare and Medicaid. 
• Northern Shenandoah Valley Regional Commission to invest in organizations advancing 
mobile and hybrid care for patients in the Shenandoah Valley. 
• VA Center for Health Innovation to evaluate and monitor the program.
