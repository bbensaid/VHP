GCR 26-089 
PROPOSED 
 
Discontinuation of the Hospital Global Budget Payment Model 
 
Policy Summary:   
The Vermont Medicaid program implemented a voluntary hospital global budget (HGB) 
payment model in CY2026, which allows participating hospitals to be paid prospectively for 
services covered under the HGB. Eligible hospitals include short-term acute inpatient hospitals 
and Critical Access Hospitals (CAHs) in Vermont.  
 
The Agency of Human Services received notice from the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid 
Services (CMS) on July 7, 2026, notifying the State that the Medicaid hospital global budget 
(HGB) implemented by the Department of Vermont Health Access (DVHA) has not been 
approved by CMS in accordance with the requirements in Vermont’s Global Commitment to 
Health section 1115(a) demonstration (Project Number 11-W-00194/1) special terms and 
conditions (STCs), and that the State is not compliant with STCs 6.3(a) and 6.4(i)(iii). As a result, 
CMS requires that the State take corrective actions to: 
1. Cease making HGB payments to participating hospitals within 60 days of the date of the 
notice and begin making payments in line with the payment methodology used for 
providers who are not participating and are being paid the Medicaid HGB. 
2. Reprocess all HGB payments made to participating hospitals since January 1, 2026, in 
line with the payment methodology used for non-HGB participating providers. 
 
DVHA will cease making HGB payments to participating hospitals and begin making payments 
through its fee-for-service reimbursement for hospitals on July 31, 2026.  
 
DVHA will begin reprocessing all HGB payments made to participating hospitals from January 1, 
2026, by recouping HGB payments and reprocessing all associated zero-paid claims as fee-for-
service, on or prior to January 7, 2027. 
 
Effective Date: 
July 31, 2026 
 
Authority/Legal Basis: 
Medicaid State Plan
