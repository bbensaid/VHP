Comparison to Medicare
Medicare payment system used as a benchmark, not as a price goal
• Medicare prices and methods are empirically based and transparent
• Benchmarking to Medicare allows employers to compare to the largest 
purchaser in the world, but also to compare hospitals, systems, 
states, & service types across years

comparisons
