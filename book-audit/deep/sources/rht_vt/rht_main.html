Vermont's Rural Health Transformation Program

Vermont’s Rural Health Transformation (RHT) Program is a five-year federal funding initiative designed to promote bold, innovative ideas that make care more accessible, higher quality, and better tailored to meet the needs of rural communities.  

The RHT Program is funded by the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS). Vermont’s Year 1 (Federal Fiscal year 2026) award is $195 million. Drawing on Vermont's rich history of health care reform and stakeholder engagement, Vermont has set concrete goals and a comprehensive approach for how to leverage this funding opportunity. Vermont’ RHT Program Goals are: 

  1. Strengthening Vermont’s Rural Health Care Workforce; 
  2. Increasing Access to Timely Care Across Rural Communities; and 
  3. Leveraging Innovative Strategies to Increase Quality and Reduce Health Care Costs. 

To achieve these goals, Vermont has identified 35 activities across five Strategic Initiatives.  

Vermont’s Year 1 RHT funding is fully obligated​
$195M​

Vermont has successfully obligated its $195 million Year 1 award, meaning the State has committed the full amount to specific activities and documented those commitments with CMS. For more details visit the RHT Year 1 Awards and Contracts webpage. ​
