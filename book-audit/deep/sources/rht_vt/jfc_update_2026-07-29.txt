What is the Rural Health Transformation Program?
• On November 1, 2025, the State of Vermont submitted its Rural Health Transformation (RHT) 
Fund application to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services’ Center for Medicare and 
Medicaid Services (CMS). 
• This funding opportunity came about as part of the H.R. 1 reconciliation bill. Nationally, the 
program provides $50 billion over five years (2026–2030). 
• Each year, $10 billion will be awarded to states.
• Vermont’s exact allocation is determined by the federal government after reviewing our 
application and subsequent reporting.
• RHT is a cooperative agreement with CMS
