1. Summary of Projects 
The JFO IT consultant monitors the following projects. 
Unemployment Insurance Modernization Project 
 
• In 2022, GA authorized expenditure of $27M from the Technology Modernization Special Fund 
for the UI Modernization project to be released as follows: 
• A JFO review was completed in January 2024 and JITOC approved the release of $30 million 
appropriated for the FY23 budget 
• The total funds available for this project are $52.3 million from appropriations in FY22, FY23, 
an increase in VDOL operating budget to support this project, and savings from costs used to 
support the old system. 
• The total cost for the UI System's 25-month implementation period and three years of 
management and operations is $45.3 million. (Note: A contract amendment was processed 
within the last month. I do not have the final numbers.) 
Current Status 
 
The UI Modernization project is on track for completion in Summer 2026. 
 
Last fall, at a meeting with JITOC, the Vermont Department of Labor (VDOL), there was a 
discussion about the importance of stakeholder engagement. After the meeting, the VDOL team 
brought in Vermont Legal Aid, National Association of State Workforce Agencies (NASWA), and 
HireAbility (formerly Voc Rehap) for testing. Commissioner Smith said the stakeholders' feedback 
was similar, and they were able to accommodate most of it. 
VDOL has also engaged with external users (businesses and current UI Claimants about the 
project and what to expect. They recently held a virtual town hall with VLCT, attended by 90 
participants. They have more town halls planned over the next few months. 
 
VDOL leadership is engaged daily with FAST and weekly with ADS and FAST to ensure they are 
tracking issues uncovered during User Acceptance testing.

2. Summary of Upcoming JFO IT Reviews 
Integrated Eligibility and Enrollment (IE&E) System Modernization 
• Agency of Human Services/ADS 
• Purpose is to provide Vermonters with a centralized, simplified, and user-friendly experience for applying 
and managing State benefits, as well as secure enrollment, personalized access, streamlined 
applications, and comprehensive customer service. 
• A competitive bid process was conducted, and a preferred vendor selection is complete.. 
• As a requirement of funding, two federal agencies must approved the contract. Approval is imminent. 
• The project will undergo a CIO's Independent Review, per 3 V.S.A. § 3303(d). Once this is completed, a JFO 
IT Review will occur for this project 
• JFO expected a request for State match funds in the current year, but it was not submitted. The Project 
Team indicated that requests for information about the source of funds for the State Match should be 
directed to Tracy O'Connell, the AHS Chief Financial Officer.
