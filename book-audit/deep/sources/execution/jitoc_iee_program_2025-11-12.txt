5Current Challenges
For Vermonters
• Repeated data entry across programs
• No unified application or renewal process
• Limited mobile access and language support
• Risk of coverage gaps due to confusing notices
For State Staff
• Manual, time-consuming processes
• Siloed systems and fragmented data
• High maintenance costs and compliance risk

12The Risk of Delay
• Compliance & Funding: Federal standards, 
recoupment risk
• Operational Fragility: Aging technology, sourcing 
skilled staff for legacy system support
• Technical Fragility: Complex programming 
hinders timely updates and responsiveness
• Service Impact: Benefit disruption, accessibility gaps​
“ACCESS Mainframe risk is widely recognized – delay 
increases exposure”

13VT-IES: Investing in People & Modern Systems
• Design & Technology: Tailored for Vermont, 
reuse of state technology investments
• Technical Debt Reduction: Consolidating two 
legacy systems into a single, modern platform
• Efficiency & Integrity: Configurable rules engine, 
statewide health data analytics, program integrity
• Federal Partnership: CMS/FNS co-funding, 
strategic validation
“Smart investment, long-term value”

14Timeline – Why it Takes Time
RFP Development (Jan 2022 – Sept 2024)
• 3 RFIs to gather vendor input and validate feasibility
• Federal partner collaboration with CMS and FNS
Procurement (Oct 2024 – July 2026)
• RFP posting, scoring, demos, contracting, federal and state reviews
Design, Development, Implementation (2026 – 2030): 
• Phased delivery over 3-4 years with early wins
“Built for sustainability, not speed”

15Vermont’s Modernization Journey
What’s Different This Time?
• Past Challenges: VHC launch, rushed timelines, lack of user support 
and training.
• Past Successes: System stability, security compliance, risk mitigation
• New Approach: New IE&E leadership since 2022, change 
management focus and stakeholder coordination.
• Federal Alignment: Procurement rigor, outcome-driven design
“Lessons learned, applied with discipline”
