Bernie Sanders plans to implement his version of the universal healthcare system over four years. In the first year, the eligibility age would drop; coverage would expand to include dental, hearing, and vision care; and it would provide coverage for eligible children. (Lovelace Jr., 11/18) Modern Healthcare: UnitedHealth, Humana, Aetna Calm Investors On Medicare Advantage Growth is no longer a bad word for Medicare Advantage insurance company investors. Major Medicare Advantage insurers UnitedHealth Group subsidiary UnitedHealthcare, Humana, CVS Health’s Aetna and Elevance Health that reported third-quarter earnings in recent weeks indicated that they expect modest growth during the annual sign-up campaign, which began Oct. 15 and runs until Dec. 7. This outlook represents a reversal from companies’ previous projections of declining or flat enrollment for 2027. Until recently, economic researchers were cheering an encouraging trend: Older Americans were staying in the labor force longer and waiting longer to claim their Social Security benefits, aiming to enhance their financial stability in retirement. Not everyone, of course. Despite financial advisers’ counsel and economists’ hopes, most people still claim their benefits before reaching their designated full retirement age, though that usually means lower monthly payments for life.
