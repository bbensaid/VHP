Federal Medicaid Changes Impacting Vermont

Medicaid is a government-funded health insurance plan for income-eligible people and people who are categorically eligible. In Vermont, Medicaid is run by the Department of Vermont Health Access. There are different types of Medicaid. Medicaid covers medical care and prescriptions drugs. To learn more about Medicaid coverage and what you may be eligible for, explore the four health coverage options below.
